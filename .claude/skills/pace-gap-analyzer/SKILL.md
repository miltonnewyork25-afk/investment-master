# Pace Gap Analyzer

## What this skill does
This skill asks whether the opportunity is still early enough.

A trend can be correct and still be a bad trade if the market has already crowded in.

This skill compares:
- business realization
- market diffusion
- capacity expansion or constraint relief

## Default workflow
1. Decide where the business is in its realization path.
   Examples:
   - qualification
   - design win
   - order
   - backlog
   - volume ramp
   - margin improvement
   - cash flow inflection

2. Decide where the market is in its diffusion path.
   Examples:
   - specialists know
   - company is talking about it
   - sell-side is updating
   - financial media is spreading it
   - social/retail crowding is visible

3. Decide whether scarcity is still intact or starting to ease.

4. Conclude whether the setup feels:
   - early
   - validating
   - accelerating
   - crowded
   - overextended

## What good use looks like
A good output should explain:
- whether there is still timing edge
- whether realization leads diffusion
- whether the setup is too obvious already
- whether the next phase is still ahead

## Key reminder
Price moves alone are not enough.
Use operating evidence, ecosystem evidence, and crowding evidence together.

## Flexibility
Not all opportunities move through the same clock.
Adapt the sequence when needed.
