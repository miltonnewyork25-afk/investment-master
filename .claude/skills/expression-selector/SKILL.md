# Expression Selector

## What this skill does
This skill decides where the best expression of the trend sits.

The obvious name is not always the best name.

This skill exists to choose among:
- leader
- second-order beneficiary
- upstream bottleneck owner
- hidden owner
- bridge owner
- duration owner
- no-trade

The goal is not just to find the most logical beneficiary.
The goal is to find the best current expression of the opportunity.

## Default workflow
1. List the obvious expression.
2. List second-order and upstream alternatives.
3. Ask whether a bridge owner offers a better setup.
4. Ask whether a duration owner offers a better setup.
5. Compare which layer has:
   - better ownership
   - less crowding
   - cleaner timing
   - better remaining upside
   - better durability
6. State whether the best answer is:
   - leader
   - second-order beneficiary
   - upstream owner
   - hidden owner
   - bridge owner
   - duration owner
   - no-trade

## Expression types to compare

### 1. Core leader
The most obvious and direct beneficiary.

### 2. Second-order beneficiary
Benefits from the core ramp but is one step removed.

### 3. Upstream bottleneck owner
Controls a key constrained input or enabling node.

### 4. Hidden owner
Has real economics but is not widely recognized as part of the trend.

### 5. Bridge owner
Connects multiple propagation chains or thematic buckets.
May have a better setup than the obvious theme leader because it monetizes multiple demand flows.

### 6. Duration owner
May not move first, but can monetize the installed base, maintenance, replacement, or long-tail economics better than the early winner.

### 7. No-trade
The trend may be real, but the current expression may be too crowded, too early, too speculative, or too fully priced.

## What to compare
For each candidate expression, ask:

- Is ownership cleaner here?
- Is the market more crowded here?
- Is timing better here?
- Is the expectation gap better here?
- Is the economics more durable here?
- Is this company benefiting from one wave or from multiple linked waves?
- Is the market underestimating lifecycle or installed-base monetization here?

## What good use looks like
A good output should make clear:

- what the obvious expression is
- why that obvious expression is or is not the best one
- whether a bridge owner is more attractive
- whether a duration owner is more attractive
- whether the current best action is actually no-trade

## Key reminder
Correct trend does not mean correct expression.

### 2. Bridge owners are easy to miss
If a company monetizes several real chains at once, it may have a better setup than a pure-play theme name.

### 3. Duration owners are often underappreciated
Markets often overfocus on early deployment and underfocus on long-tail monetization.

### 4. The best expression can change over time
Early in a cycle, the best expression may be:
- core leader
- chokepoint owner

Later, it may be:
- bridge owner
- duration owner

### 5. No-trade is valid
If every relevant expression is too crowded, say so.

## Flexibility
If the best answer is not one of the standard labels, explain the hybrid.

For example:
- near-term best expression is the chokepoint owner, but long-duration best expression is the duration owner
- the bridge owner offers the best risk-adjusted expression even though it is not the thematic pure-play
