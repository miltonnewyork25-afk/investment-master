# Part I — How The Framework Was Forced Into Existence

The framework is not a clean a-priori design. It is the residue of recurring research errors. Each principle exists because we repeatedly ran into the same failure.

## Error 1 — 传统选股思路总是太晚

Most research starts from:
- 股价涨了很多
- 财报很好
- EPS 爆了
- 毛利率提升了
- 某个主题很热门
- 想找"类似某个牛股"的下一只

These are not useless. But they share one problem:

**They are mostly results, not causes.**

If you always start from results, you are already behind the market. The market sees results first.

**→ Principle 1: 必须从因果源头开始,而不是从结果开始.**

This is why the framework leads with Source / Cause / Forced demand — not financial factors.

---

## Error 2 — 趋势是真的, 不代表这只股票值得买

The second big trap. You often find:
- 主题对
- 行业方向对
- 需求是真的
- 但股票还是不值得买

Why? Because "trend correct" solves only one thing: **the world is indeed changing this way.**

But investing requires two harder answers:
- 这段利润最后归谁?
- 市场现在是不是已经买过头了?

**FORM case (HBM exposure)** showed this clearly: HBM demand was real, narrative was real, but profit attribution and capital return didn't synchronize. "Industry logic correct" ≠ "this stock worth buying now."

**→ Principle 2: 不要只问谁受益, 要问谁真正拥有利润.**

This is why the framework has Owner / Profit ownership / Return ownership.

---

## Error 3 — 不是所有受益者都一样

Going further, even "owner" isn't enough.

In any supply chain, beneficiaries exist at many layers:
- 龙头
- 二阶受益
- 上游材料
- 关键设备
- 认证方
- 后市场
- 隐性控制点

Market habit: **buy the most visible, best-story, most viral layer first.**

But the most visible layer is often not the best risk-reward. What you actually need to research:
- 哪个环节最难绕开
- 哪个环节最慢扩产
- 哪个环节最难替代
- 哪个环节客户切换最难

This is **chokepoint thinking** — not moat in the general sense, but:
**增长必须穿过的窄门.**

**→ Principle 3: 要找不是"最显眼"的受益者, 而是"最难绕开的节点".**

This is why the framework has Constraint node / Chokepoint / Restricted node.

---

## Error 4 — 发现一条趋势, 不等于现在还能赚钱

Many analyses stop at:
- 这个趋势是真的
- 公司也会受益
- 逻辑没问题

But investing isn't a truth question — it's a **timing question**. You must keep asking:
- 现在是刚开始, 还是已经扩散?
- 现在是 business 先走, 还是市场先走?
- 现在是兑现早期, 还是末端泡沫?
- 现在买的是"未来利润", 还是"别人已经买过的预期"?

This is why the 双时钟 / 三时钟 exist:
- realization clock
- diffusion clock
- expansion clock

**→ Principle 4: 真正的机会来自现实兑现速度快于市场认知扩散速度.**

Not the trend itself produces alpha — **the gap between realization and diffusion produces alpha.**

This is why the framework has Pace / Diffusion / Expectation layering / Timing edge.

---

## Error 5 — 不是所有需求都是高质量需求

Even "demand exists" is not enough.

Demand can be split into:
- 可选需求
- 半强制需求
- 强制需求

The best investment windows often come from:
- 不修不行
- 不换不行
- 不建不行
- 不升级不行
- 不配套不行

Forced demand has unique properties:
- 持续性更强
- 对景气波动更不敏感
- 更容易形成真实传导
- 更容易逼出 bottleneck
- 更容易让 owner 拿到利润

**→ Principle 5: 最强的高增长机会, 通常来自强制性支出, 而不是可选性支出.**

This is why FROG's F leads the filter.

# Part II — The 8 Core Thinking Modes

Compressed to 8 first-principle thinking modes. These are load-bearing. Rules can be derived from them; they cannot be derived from rules.

## 思维 1 · 结果不是起点, 因果才是起点

Do not start from price, earnings, or valuation. First ask:

**什么力量在推动这件事发生?**

This is the most fundamental starting point.

## 思维 2 · 需求不重要, 强制性需求才重要

Not "will people buy" but:

**是不是不买不行.**

Forced demand quality >> discretionary demand quality.

## 思维 3 · 趋势不等于利润, 利润必须有 owner

Industry benefit ≠ shareholder benefit. Continue asking:

**这段钱到底归谁?**

This is one of the framework's most valuable questions.

## 思维 4 · 最好的机会常常藏在受限节点, 而不是最热节点

The market rushes into "story centers." True long windows are in:
- 受限产能
- 认证壁垒
- 替换件
- 后市场
- 材料卡点
- 长周期基础设施

**必须经过, 但不容易被看见的节点.**

## 思维 5 · 高增长股票的本质, 不是增长快, 而是增长难以被替代

This may be the most important single insight.

真正值得高估值或高溢价的增长, 不是单纯高增速, 而是:
- 别人短期追不上
- 扩产很慢
- 客户切换很难
- 认证周期很长
- 基础设施已占位

**高增长的本质, 不是增速, 而是"稀缺的持续增长".**

## 思维 6 · 最重要的不是趋势对不对, 而是时钟谁领先

Same trend, different timing:
- 有人买得早
- 有人买得正好
- 有人买得太晚

差别不在逻辑, 在于:

**现实世界的兑现, 和市场世界的扩散, 谁跑在前面.**

This is the essence of Pace.

## 思维 7 · 市场不是总看错方向, 更多时候是看错层级

Often the market knows the trend but:
- 买错了表达
- 买错了 owner
- 买错了时点
- 买错了利润层

Alpha often comes not from "discovering an unknown theme" but from:

**发现市场正在错误层级上定价.**

This is the essence of Expectation Layering.

## 思维 8 · 好框架不是限制思考, 而是保护思考

The framework is not a template-filling exercise. Its purpose is to prevent the most expensive errors:
- 从结果开始
- 把受益当 owner
- 把热门当机会
- 把收入当利润
- 把叙事当兑现
- 把正确趋势当正确股票

**降低大错概率的思考骨架.**

Not the answer itself. A guardrail that lets the agent keep freedom without drifting.

# Part III — Why This Is Closer To Finding Real High-Growth Stocks

Market's typical understanding of "high growth":
- 收入增速高
- 订单增长快
- 叙事空间大
- 估值溢价高

This framework decomposes "high growth" further. **Real high-quality growth requires all five:**

### 1. 增长来自强制性源头
Not icing on the cake — must happen.

### 2. 增长必须经过受限节点
So it is not quickly replicated and diluted.

### 3. 增长的利润归属清晰
Not diffuse benefit — concentrated ownership.

### 4. 增长仍处在现实兑现领先于市场扩散的阶段
Not fully bid up yet.

### 5. 增长可以转化为资本回报
Not just revenue — ROIC / FCF / shareholder value.

Therefore the framework is not searching for:

**"看起来增长快的股票"**

It is searching for:

**"被强制性需求推动、经过受限节点、利润归属清晰、兑现早于扩散、且仍有剩余预期差的增长 owner."**

Rarer. Harder to find. But closer to what you actually want.

# Part IV — Agent Invocation Block

(Use this block when instructing agent / Codex / research model to operate in this framework.)

```
SCOPE-A v2 / CMTD+M does not treat growth as a surface metric.
It treats growth as an economic phenomenon that must be explained causally.

A high-quality growth opportunity is not simply a company with fast revenue growth.
It is a situation where forced or durable demand is pushing through a restricted
node, economic ownership is concentrated, realization is underway, and the market
has not yet fully priced the correct layer of that realization.

This framework exists because most analysis starts too late:
from earnings, price, valuation, or theme popularity.
We instead start from source, constraint, ownership, pace, and expectations.

The framework is built around several core truths:
- outcomes are downstream; causes matter more than results
- demand quality matters more than demand quantity
- trend exposure is not the same as economic ownership
- bottlenecks matter more than visibility
- timing edge comes from the gap between realization and diffusion
- the best opportunities are often mislayered, not completely undiscovered

The purpose of the framework is not to constrain discovery.
Its purpose is to protect causal thinking while preserving freedom.
It should help the agent discover better owners, better expression layers,
earlier windows, and more durable mispricings.
```

# Part V — One-Sentence Compression

The framework's essence in a single sentence:

> **它的本质, 是用"因果 + 卡点 + 利润归属 + 时间差 + 错层定价"去取代"财务结果 + 热门叙事 + 静态估值"的传统选股思维.**

That is why it is closer to finding truly high-quality, high-growth stocks.
