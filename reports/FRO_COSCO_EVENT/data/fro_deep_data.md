## 4. Fredriksen Ship Transactions (Jan 2026)

### The $2B Fleet Swap Deal (Announced January 8, 2026)

This is the single most important corporate action in FRO's recent history — a **related-party fleet modernization** transaction with John Fredriksen's Hemen Holding.

#### Leg 1: Sale of 8 Older VLCCs
| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Vessels sold | 8 first-generation ECO VLCCs |
| Build year | 2015-2016 |
| Total sale price | **$831.5M** (~$104M/vessel) |
| Net cash proceeds | ~$486M (after debt repayment) |
| Delivery timing | Q1 2026 |
| Buyer | Thenamaris (Greek shipping company) |

#### Leg 2: Acquisition of 9 Newbuild VLCCs
| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Vessels acquired | 9 latest-gen ECO VLCCs with scrubbers |
| Seller | Hemen Holding (Fredriksen affiliate) |
| Total purchase price | **$1,224M** (~$136M/vessel) |
| Shipyards | 6 at Hengli, 3 at Dalian (both China) |
| Delivery schedule | 7 in 2026 (from Q3), 1 in Q1 2027, 1 in Q2 2027 |

#### Economics of the Related-Party Deal
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Hemen's original order price | ~$118M/vessel |
| Sale to Frontline | $136M/vessel |
| Hemen's nominal profit per ship | ~$18M |
| Hemen's total profit on 9 ships | ~$162M |
| Net cash outflow for Frontline | ~$738M ($1,224M - $486M net proceeds) |

- **Related-party concern**: Hemen Holding is Frontline's largest shareholder (controlled by John Fredriksen). The $18M/vessel markup is modest vs. newbuild market prices but still a transfer from public shareholders to Fredriksen's private entity.
- **Strategic rationale**: Replaces 10-year-old vessels with brand-new ships, lowering average fleet age and opex. The sold VLCCs are at peak resale value before age-related depreciation accelerates.
- Riviera Maritime described it as a **"win-win"** for Frontline (younger fleet) and Hemen (capital recycling + profit).

Source: [Riviera Maritime - $2Bn VLCC Deal](https://www.rivieramm.com/news-content-hub/news-content-hub/frontline-seals-us2bn-vlcc-fleet-modernisation-deals-87365), [gCaptain - Fleet Overhaul](https://gcaptain.com/frontline-rolls-out-2-billion-fleet-overhaul-with-strategic-vessel-swap/), [Marine Log](https://www.marinelog.com/news/frontline-sells-eight-older-vlccs-and-buys-nine-newbuilds/), [Breakbulk News - Thenamaris](https://breakbulk.news/thenamaris-eyes-1-billion-tanker-fleet-from-frontline-in-bold-market-move/)

## 6. Key Takeaways for Event-Driven Analysis

1. **Fleet is young and getting younger**: 7.5-year average age, replacing 2015-16 builds with 2026-27 newbuilds. Lowest-cost operator advantage.
2. **Q1 2026 rate spike is massive**: $107,100/day VLCC vs. $74,200 in Q4 — a +44% QoQ jump. If sustained, EPS could far exceed the $3.11 consensus.
3. **Related-party governance risk**: Fredriksen's Hemen earned ~$162M on the fleet swap. Minority shareholders bear the cost.
4. **Analysts overwhelmingly bullish**: 100% Buy/Strong Buy, median target $32 (but stock may already be near/above this given YTD rally).
5. **Tanker supply tightening**: Venezuela seizures + sanctions on dark fleet + aging global fleet = structural support for rates.
6. **Dividend machine**: $1.03/share in Q4 alone, 81% payout, ~11% annualized yield attracts income investors.
