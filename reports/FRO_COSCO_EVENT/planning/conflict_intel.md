## 1. Iran-US Conflict: Military Situation & Shipping Disruption

### Timeline of Key Events
- **Feb 28, 2026**: US and Israel launch coordinated military strikes on Iran, including killing of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. Strikes targeted command centers and missile silos.
- **Mar 2, 2026**: IRGC declares Strait of Hormuz "closed" — begins mining the strait and deploying anti-ship cruise missiles along coastline. First attacks on commercial vessels attempting transit.
- **Mar 5, 2026**: IRGC announces selective blockade — strait closed only to ships from US, Israel, and Western allies. "Friendly" nations (China, India, Turkey, Muslim-majority countries) offered permission-based transits through Iranian territorial waters.
- **Mar 11, 2026**: Large wave of attacks — at least 3 vessels sustain damage in a single day. 20 crew of Thailand-flagged Mayuree Naree rescued by Oman; 3 missing.
- **Mar 14, 2026**: First full day with zero AIS-confirmed commercial crossings in either direction.
- **Mar 16, 2026**: US announces Operation Guardian Aegis — naval convoy escort mission. "Mother duck" style with Aegis destroyers surrounding tanker clusters. First convoy expected within 48-72 hours.

## 3. War Risk Premium & Shipping Insurance

### Premium Escalation Timeline
| Period | Rate | Basis | Source |
|--------|------|-------|--------|
| Pre-conflict | 0.125% - 0.25% of hull value | Per transit | Wikipedia / Al Jazeera |
| Early March (initial spike) | 0.2% - 0.4% of hull value | Per transit | Wikipedia |
| Mar 5-7 (new contracts) | 1% of hull replacement value | Renewable every 7 days | Caixin / S&P Global |
| Mar 9+ (Hormuz transit) | 1.5% - 3% of hull value | Per transit | Bloomberg / Insurance Journal |
| Peak (some reports) | ~5% of hull value | Per transit | Bloomberg |

### Dollar Impact
- For a VLCC worth ~$100M: insurance cost surged from ~$125,000-250,000 to **$1.5M-5M per transit**
- This is a **12-40x increase** in insurance cost alone
- Comparable to Iran-Iraq Tanker War of 1980s (premiums reached 5% for extended period; hundreds of vessels struck)

### Insurer Actions
- **Mar 5, 2026**: Gard, Skuld, NorthStandard, London P&I Club, American Club all cancelled war risk cover effective immediately
- Major marine war risk providers **scrapped cover entirely** for Persian Gulf operations
- Cover effectively unavailable at any price for a period

### Government Response
- **Chubb** named lead underwriter for US government-led insurance program
- Working with **US International Development Finance Corp (DFC)**
- **$20 billion program** to insure ships making Hormuz transits
- Goal: get oil tankers and commercial traffic moving again

**Confidence: HIGH** — Bloomberg, S&P Global, Al Jazeera, Insurance Journal, CNBC all corroborate. Some variance in exact premium levels reflects different vessel classes and timing.

### Key Contradiction
Premium levels vary significantly across sources (1% to 5% of hull value). This likely reflects: (a) different time windows, (b) different vessel classes, (c) transit vs. regional cover, (d) rapidly changing conditions. The 5% figure may represent peak/extreme quotes while 1-3% is the "market" rate for new contracts.

### China-Iran Oil Corridor (The Key Dynamic)
Iran has continued exporting oil through Hormuz **exclusively to China**:
- **11.7 million barrels** of Iranian crude shipped through strait since war began (as of Mar 15), ALL headed to China
- Iran operating a **selective blockade**: Chinese-flagged/claimed vessels get safe passage
- Ships rerouting through Iranian territorial waters with "permission-based transits to friendly countries"

- **45% of China's oil** normally transits Strait of Hormuz
- China pressing Tehran for guaranteed safe passage for its vessels

### COSCO Historical Sanctions Risk
- **Sep 2019**: US Treasury sanctioned COSCO Shipping Tanker (Dalian) for transporting Iranian oil
- **2020**: OFAC lifted sanctions on COSCO Shipping Energy Transportation
- **Current**: No new sanctions on main COSCO entity, but the company is walking a tightrope between US sanctions compliance and China's strategic interest in Iranian oil

### Shadow Fleet vs. Legitimate Fleet Distinction
COSCO Shipping Energy (1138.HK / 600026.SS) operates the **legitimate fleet** — publicly listed, subject to sanctions compliance. The ~430 tankers in Iranian trade are predominantly shadow fleet operators (62% falsely flagged, 87% sanctioned). COSCO benefits from tight legitimate market supply (shadow fleet absorption) without directly participating in sanctioned trades.

## Cross-Topic Synthesis: Key Contradictions & Open Questions

### Contradictions Noted
1. **Insurance premiums**: Range from 1% to 5% of hull value across sources — reflects different timing, vessel classes, and coverage types
2. **Shadow fleet size**: Ukraine catalog says 1,337; S&P Global says 978 tankers >27K DWT; Windward says ~1,100. Different methodologies and inclusion criteria.
3. **Strait status**: "Zero crossings" on Mar 14, but Iran simultaneously exporting 11.7M bbl to China through the same strait. Resolution: Iran's own tankers + "friendly" flagged vessels continue; Western/neutral commercial traffic stopped.
4. **EIA outlook**: Expects Brent back to $70/bbl by year-end, implying conflict resolution. This contrasts with military reality of mine-seeded strait and 21 ship attacks.

### Open Questions for Further Research
1. **Duration**: How long can Iran sustain the blockade against US naval pressure (Guardian Aegis)?
2. **Escalation risk**: Will convoy operations trigger direct Iran-US naval confrontation?
3. **SPR releases**: US/IEA strategic petroleum reserve releases — scale and sustainability?
4. **Replacement barrels**: Can West Africa, US shale, Brazil fully substitute for stranded ME Gulf barrels?
5. **Insurance normalization**: How quickly will premiums decline if convoy operations succeed?
6. **FRO earnings leverage**: What is the specific earnings sensitivity per $10K/day VLCC rate change?
7. **COSCO dual exposure**: How does COSCO balance benefiting from tight market vs. sanctions compliance risk if China-Iran corridor deepens?
