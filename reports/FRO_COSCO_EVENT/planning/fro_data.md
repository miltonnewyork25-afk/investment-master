## 7. Notable Patterns & Analysis

### Cyclicality (Defining Characteristic)
- FRO is a **pure-play tanker rate cyclical**. Revenue swung from $749M (2021 trough) to $2.05B (2024 peak) — a 2.7x range
- Net income swung from **-$15M to +$656M** — essentially binary (profitable or not)
- EBITDA margin ranged from 28% (trough) to 59% (peak) — operating leverage is extreme
- Q4 2025 alone ($228M net income) was 15x the Q1 2025 result ($33M) — even intra-year swings are violent

### Dividend Variability
- FRO pays **variable dividends tied to earnings** — no fixed dividend commitment
- Dividend per share: $0 (2021) -> $0.16 (2022) -> $2.87 (2023) -> $1.95 (2024) -> $0.93 (2025)
- Payout ratio: 0% -> 7% -> 97% -> 88% -> 55% — shifted to near-full payout during upcycle
- **Current yield ~4.3%** based on last 12 months, but this is highly rate-dependent

### Fleet Expansion Cycle
- **2023-2024**: Massive fleet investment ($2.55B CapEx) — Euronav merger completed, making FRO the world's largest listed tanker company
- **2025**: Investment phase complete (CapEx only $13M), pivoted to **debt paydown** ($662M repaid)
- Fleet grew from ~70 vessels (2021) to significantly larger post-Euronav (~80+ VLCCs + Suezmax + LR2)
- D&A doubled from $160M (2021) to $328M (2025) reflecting the expanded fleet

### Capital Structure
- Highly leveraged but manageable: Net Debt/EBITDA 3.1x, declining
- **Zero beta** (-0.007) — FRO trades on tanker rates, not equity market direction
- No share buybacks ever — all shareholder returns via dividends
- Interest expense is a major P&L item: $233M in 2025 (12% of revenue)

### Valuation Context

- But forward P/E is 13.3x — market expects earnings to decline further from the FY2025 level
