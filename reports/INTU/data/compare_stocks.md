## Key Observations

### Valuation
- **INTU trades at a premium P/E (29.8x)** vs ADBE (14.4x), CRM (25.0x), and SPY (26.0x)
- INTU's premium is ~2x ADBE and ~4x HRB
- Among software peers, INTU commands the highest P/E

### Profitability (ROE)
- **ADBE leads with 58.8% ROE** — significantly higher than INTU (23.5%) and CRM (12.4%)
- INTU's ROE is respectable but behind ADBE's capital efficiency
- CRM's lower ROE reflects its larger asset base from acquisitions

### Price-to-Book
- INTU and ADBE have similar P/B (~11x), suggesting comparable intangible asset intensity
- CRM's lower P/B (3.4x) reflects its massive goodwill base relative to market cap
- HRB's extreme P/B is an artifact of near-zero equity
