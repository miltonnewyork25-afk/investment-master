## ESG → Sustainability & Climate Rename
- Q1 2025: Renamed from "ESG and Climate" to "Sustainability and Climate"
- Interpretation: De-emphasizing "ESG" label (political headwinds in US) while preserving substance
- Revenue growth slowed from ~15-20% (2022) to ~6% (2025) — confirming H2 "保险化" thesis
- But climate-specific products growing faster within the segment
