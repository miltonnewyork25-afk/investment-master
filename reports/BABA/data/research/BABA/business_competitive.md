## Part 2: Competitive Landscape

### 2.1 China E-Commerce Market

**Market Size**: ~USD 1.53 trillion (2025), projected to reach USD 2.52 trillion by 2030.

#### Market Share by Platform (GMV-based, 2024-2025 estimates)

| Platform | GMV Share (2024 est.) | GMV (RMB T) | Trend | Core Differentiator |
|----------|-----------------------|-------------|-------|---------------------|
| **Taobao + Tmall** (Alibaba) | ~40-44% | ~9-10 | Declining from 52% (2021) | Broadest SKU; 88VIP loyalty; Quanzhantui ad system |
| **PDD (Pinduoduo)** | ~19-22% | ~5.2 | Rising sharply (from 7% in 2019) | Extreme price; social/group buying; gamification |
| **JD.com** | ~17-20% | ~4+ | Stable/slight decline | Self-operated logistics; quality assurance; 3C/appliances |
| **Douyin (TikTok China)** | ~12-15% | ~3.5 (2024), +34% YoY | Fastest growth | Livestream/short video commerce; content-driven discovery |
| **Kuaishou** | ~4-5% | ~1.2 | Growing | Similar to Douyin; lower-tier cities |
| **Others** (Xiaohongshu, Vipshop, etc.) | ~5-8% | — | Fragmented | Niche/vertical |

**Key Competitive Dynamics**:

1. **Alibaba vs. PDD**: PDD's "price-first" algorithm and Temu international expansion erode Alibaba's price-sensitive user base. Alibaba responded with "Hundred-Billion Subsidy" program and Taobao price-competitive features. CMR take-rate increase (+10% in Q2 FY2026) shows monetization recovery, but GMV growth lags PDD.

2. **Alibaba vs. Douyin**: Douyin's GMV grew 34% YoY (Aug 2024 - Jul 2025), overtaking JD.com as #3 platform. Content-driven commerce captures discovery/impulse purchases that traditionally went to Taobao. Alibaba's response: Taobao content feed + short video investment.

3. **Alibaba vs. JD**: JD launched food delivery (Feb 2025) reaching 25M daily orders, directly attacking Ele.me. The two are also competing in instant retail. JD's self-operated model provides quality assurance advantage in 3C and groceries.

4. **Quick Commerce Battle (2025)**: Industry burn rate estimated >$4B in Q2 2025. Alibaba combined Ele.me + Taobao Instant Commerce reaching 40M daily orders vs. Meituan's 90M daily orders. Alibaba's quick commerce revenue grew 60% in Q3 FY2026.

### 2.2 China Cloud Market

**Market Size**: $11.6B (Q1 2025) → $12.4B (Q2 2025) → $13.4B (Q3 2025), growing 16% → 21% → 24% YoY, driven by AI adoption.

#### Cloud Infrastructure Market Share (Mainland China, 2025)

| Provider | Q1 2025 Share | Q3 2025 Share | Trend | Strength |
|----------|--------------|--------------|-------|----------|
| **Alibaba Cloud** | 33% | 36% | Gaining | Largest public cloud; AI model ecosystem (Qwen); broadest IaaS+PaaS |
| **Huawei Cloud** | 18% | 16% | Slight decline | Government/SOE relationships; Ascend AI chips (non-NVIDIA path) |
| **Tencent Cloud** | 10% | 9% | Slight decline | Gaming/social ecosystem; WeChat Mini Programs |
| **Baidu AI Cloud** | ~8% | — | Stable | Strong in AI inference (ERNIE); autonomous driving |
| **ByteDance (Volcano Engine)** | — | Growing rapidly | Emerging | Internal workload (Douyin) + external expansion |
| **Others** (AWS China, Azure China, etc.) | ~30% | ~30% | Fragmented | Regulatory limitations for foreign providers |

*Source: Omdia cloud infrastructure market reports (Jul 2025, Feb 2026)*

**Key Cloud Competitive Dynamics**:

1. **AI as Growth Engine**: Alibaba Cloud AI-related revenue maintained triple-digit growth for 9+ consecutive quarters (through Q2 FY2026). AI revenue >20% of external customer revenue. Eddie Wu stated AI is the "core engine" of cloud business.

2. **Qwen Model Ecosystem**: Alibaba's Qwen AI model family (open-source strategy) reached 10M downloads within 4 days of application launch. Open-source approach drives cloud adoption as developers deploy on Alibaba Cloud infrastructure.

3. **Capex Race**: Alibaba's Q2 FY2026 capex increased 80% YoY, pressuring cloud margins. This mirrors the global hyperscaler capex cycle but creates near-term profitability headwinds.

4. **Government/SOE Segment**: Huawei Cloud has structural advantage in government procurement due to domestic chip supply chain (Ascend). This limits Alibaba's TAM in a significant portion of China's cloud market.

5. **Market Acceleration**: China cloud market growth accelerated from 16% (Q1) to 24% (Q3) in 2025, driven by enterprise AI workload migration from experimentation to production. Alibaba's share gain (33% → 36%) suggests it is capturing disproportionate AI demand.

### 2.3 International E-Commerce (AIDC)

**Cross-Border E-Commerce Market Share (Global, 2025)**:

| Platform | 2025 Market Share | 2023 Share | Trend | Core Markets |
|----------|------------------|------------|-------|-------------|
| **Amazon** | Dominant overall | — | Stable | US, EU, Japan |
| **Temu (PDD)** | 24% cross-border | <1% (2022) | Explosive growth | US, EU, emerging markets |
| **Shein** | 9% cross-border | ~10% | Stable | Fashion-focused; US, EU |
| **AliExpress (Alibaba)** | 8% cross-border | 9% (2024), 12% (2023) | **Declining** | EU, LATAM, Russia |
| **Trendyol (Alibaba)** | Regional leader | — | Growing | Turkey, Middle East |
| **Lazada (Alibaba)** | Regional player | — | Mixed | Southeast Asia |

*Source: Digital Commerce 360 (Jan 2026); SCMP*

**Key International Competitive Dynamics**:

1. **AliExpress vs. Temu**: AliExpress has lost significant cross-border market share (12% → 8% in two years) as Temu's fully-managed model offers better user experience and faster delivery. Alibaba's "Choice" semi-managed model is a direct response to Temu's approach.

2. **AIDC Path to Profitability**: AIDC achieved its first quarterly adjusted EBITA profit (RMB 162M) in Q2 FY2026. Management expects first full-quarter profitability in FY2027. Loss narrowing is driven by better unit economics in Choice and Trendyol scaling.

3. **De Minimis Tariff Risk**: US de minimis exemption ($800 threshold) under political scrutiny. Any change would significantly impact Temu, Shein, and AliExpress economics for direct-to-consumer cross-border shipments.

4. **Lazada vs. Shopee**: In Southeast Asia, Shopee (Sea Limited) leads in most markets. Lazada remains #2 but has narrowed the gap in some markets. Alibaba injected additional capital into Lazada to fund competition.

5. **Trendyol Strength**: Trendyol is the clear market leader in Turkey and expanding into the Middle East. It is the strongest AIDC asset by competitive position.

### 2.4 Local Services & Quick Commerce

**China Food Delivery / Local Services Market Share (2025)**:

| Platform | Food Delivery Share | Daily Orders | Trend |
|----------|-------------------|-------------|-------|
| **Meituan** | ~67-70% | 90M (Jul 2025) | Defending dominance, spending on subsidies |
| **Ele.me (Alibaba)** | ~25-28% | 40M combined with Taobao Instant Commerce | Gaining from Alibaba ecosystem integration |
| **JD.com (Food Delivery)** | ~3-5% (new entrant) | 25M (mid-2025) | Aggressive new entrant (launched Feb 2025) |

*Source: SCMP (Jul 2025); Baiguan News*

**Key Local Services Competitive Dynamics**:

1. **Three-Way War (2025)**: JD.com's entry into food delivery (Feb 2025) disrupted the Meituan-Ele.me duopoly. Industry burn rate estimated >$4B in Q2 2025 across all three players. Meituan's share remains stable at ~70% despite intense competition.

2. **Alibaba Ecosystem Integration**: Merging Ele.me into China E-commerce Group creates cross-selling: Taobao users can order food delivery directly, Ele.me users see Taobao products. Combined daily orders (Ele.me + Taobao Instant Commerce) reached 40M.

3. **Amap Profitability**: Alibaba's mapping/ride-hailing platform Amap achieved profitability in Q3 FY2026, demonstrating local services monetization potential beyond food delivery.

4. **Subsidy Sustainability**: Current subsidy intensity is unsustainable for all three players. The question is which player can afford to subsidize longest. Meituan has the strongest unit economics; Alibaba has the deepest pockets; JD has the freshest user acquisition momentum.
