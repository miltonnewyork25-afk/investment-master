## 4. Key Bull Arguments

1. **Cloud/AI as Growth Engine**: Cloud revenue growing 34% YoY with AI-related products maintaining triple-digit growth for 9+ consecutive quarters. Cloud Intelligence revenues projected to reach RMB 150B in FY2026 (+27% YoY).

2. **Massive Undervaluation vs. Peers**: P/E of 18-19x vs. peer average of 30-37x. DCF intrinsic value estimates range $260-$268 per share, implying 40%+ undervaluation.

3. **Government Tailwind**: Beijing's warmer embrace of Alibaba as key digital infrastructure partner. Alibaba Cloud's role in China's AI modernization drive creates policy moat.

4. **Qwen AI Ecosystem**: Qwen AI scaled to 58M+ users. Qwen 3.5 launch demonstrates competitive AI model capabilities. Potential for enterprise AI monetization at scale.

5. **E-commerce Stabilization**: Core China commerce showing signs of recovery. Customer Management Revenue (CMR) returned to 10% growth in H1 FY2026.

6. **Capital Return**: Active share buyback program. Manageable capital allocation despite heavy AI investment.

7. **Sum-of-Parts Discount**: Market embedding negative valuation for Cloud Intelligence Group in current pricing, suggesting significant hidden value if cloud were valued as standalone.

---

## 5. Key Bear Arguments

1. **Margin Compression**: EBITDA margin forecast to compress from 20.3% to 13.8% in FY2026. Net income expected to decline 37.5% as AI capex ramps aggressively.

2. **AI Investment Payoff Uncertainty**: Massive capex cycle with uncertain ROI timeline. Core tension: spending rising faster than monetization.

3. **E-commerce Structural Headwinds**: China consumer slowdown, competition from PDD (Pinduoduo/Temu), Douyin (TikTok) e-commerce, and JD.com. Core commerce growth only ~4-5%.

4. **Geopolitical/Regulatory Risk**: US-China tensions, potential ADR delisting risk (though diminished). Domestic regulatory environment improved but could reverse.

5. **Quick Commerce Losses**: New business initiatives (especially quick commerce) generating significant losses. Adjusted EBITA from these segments expected to decrease ~85%.

6. **Execution Risk on Restructuring**: Multiple business reorganizations (six business groups) create management complexity. Cainiao IPO withdrawal raised governance questions.

7. **Profitability vs. Growth Tradeoff**: Analysts sharply divided on whether current margin sacrifice will generate adequate future returns. Price target range of $135-$230 reflects this deep uncertainty.

## 10. Key Risks to Monitor

1. **Q3 FY2026 Earnings Reaction** (reported Feb 23, 2026): Revenue beat but significant EPS miss — watch for PT revisions post-earnings
2. **Cloud margin trajectory**: Can EBITA margin expand beyond 8-9% as AI revenues scale?
3. **Quick commerce losses**: Whether losses narrow or widen in coming quarters
4. **US-China relations**: Any escalation could impact ADR premium
5. **AI capex cycle duration**: How many more quarters of margin compression before inflection?
6. **Competition intensity**: PDD/Douyin market share dynamics in core e-commerce
