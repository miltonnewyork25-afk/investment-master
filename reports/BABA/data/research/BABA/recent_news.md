### 3.3 Valuation Implications

The original 2020 IPO valued Ant at $313B. Post-restructuring and regulatory compliance, current market estimates range from $70-150B. An IPO of even the international arm at a fraction of the original valuation would unlock significant shareholder value for BABA, which holds ~33% of Ant Group.
