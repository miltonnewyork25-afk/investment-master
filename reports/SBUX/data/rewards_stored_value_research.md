## 4. Float Valuation (DFFV Framework)
| Value Source | Annual Estimate | Method |
|-------------|----------------|--------|
| Interest on float | ~$70-80M | $1.8B × 4% risk-free rate |
| Breakage revenue | ~$200-210M | Never-redeemed funds |
| Total annual value | ~$270-290M | Sum |

**Perpetuity valuation**:
- At 10% discount, 3% growth: $270M / 7% = ~$3.9B standalone
- Buffett $1:$1 heuristic: ~$1.8B
- **Range: $1.8B-$3.9B** as independent financial asset

**vs Bank deposits**: Zero interest paid, no regulatory burden, no FDIC, ~11% annual forfeiture = NEGATIVE cost of capital
