### Key Bull vs Bear Debates

**Bull Case:**
1. **Turnaround momentum accelerating** — First positive tx growth in 8 quarters; comp trajectory +2% -> +4% in two quarters
2. **Investor Day targets "beatable"** — Multiple analysts described FY2028 EPS $3.35-$4.00 as conservative/achievable, implying EPS CAGR 15-25% from FY2026 base
3. **Store expansion optionality** — 5,000 new U.S. stores + international doubling = significant long-term revenue runway
4. **China deconsolidation simplifies story** — Removes China comp volatility from P&L; licensing/royalty stream is higher-margin and more predictable
5. **Niccol credibility** — CMG playbook track record: menu simplification, operational excellence, margin expansion

**Bear Case:**
1. **Margin paradox** — Investing to grow (labor, Green Apron, store renovations) while EPS declining 19% YoY; the "investment year" narrative could persist longer than expected
2. **Valuation disconnected from fundamentals** — Trading at ~42x FY2026E EPS ($2.30) and ~33x FY2027E; prem for a company with declining margins and 5% trailing net margin (vs 10.4% a year ago)
3. **Dividend sustainability risk** — Payout ratio ~150-200% of trailing earnings; FCF declining from ~$5.6B to ~$4.3B; no buybacks since 2024; Motley Fool flagged potential dividend cut in 2026
4. **Labor disruption** — 3,800+ baristas participated in December 2025 strike across 180+ stores/130 cities; union demands 65%+ wage increase; $240M spent on anti-union efforts since 2021
5. **Coffee commodity headwind** — Arabica peaked at multi-decade highs; SBUX reduced hedging from $1B to ~$200M, increasing raw material volatility exposure; coffee cost inflation not fully hedged for FY2026
6. **China structural decline** — Market share collapsed from 34% (2019) to ~14% (2025) as Luckin surpassed with 29,000+ stores vs SBUX 8,000; JV valuation of $4B may understate dilution of future growth

**Key Analyst Debates (Non-Consensus):**
- *"Is the 4-minute service target sustainable at scale without permanently higher labor costs?"*
- *"Does menu simplification (25-30% reduction) risk alienating power users who drove customization-heavy orders?"*
- *"Will China JV royalty income fully offset deconsolidated operating income?"*
- *"Is Niccol's $113M Year 1 compensation justified given FY2025 results?"*

### 3B. Coffee Commodity Prices (Arabica Futures)

| Period | Arabica Price ($/lb) | Context |
|--------|---------------------|---------|
| Jan 2024 | ~$1.80-$2.00 | Pre-rally baseline |

**Supply Outlook:**
- Brazil 2026 production expected to grow **17.2% YoY** to record 66.2M bags (Arabica: 44.1M bags) — CONAB Feb 5 data
- Global production projected at record **180M bags** in 2026/27 season (+8M bags YoY)

**SBUX-Specific Hedging Concern:**
- SBUX **reduced coffee hedging** from $1B in fixed-price contracts (2019) to **under $200M** at FY2025 end
- This means peak coffee costs in FY2026 as unhedged purchases are made at elevated (though declining) spot prices
- Management guided that coffee inflation headwind will **ease in H2 FY2026** as prices normalize and new-crop Brazil supply enters
- **~1/3 of the 420 bps NA margin decline** in Q1 was attributed to coffee & tariff inflation

### 3C. Minimum Wage & Labor Cost Pressures

**Current Wage Structure:**
- Starting wage in 33 states: $15.25/hr
- Median barista hourly pay: ~$14.73 (2025 data)
- Examples: Brooklyn $17.25, supervisor roles ~$19.37
- Average barista scheduled for **19 hours/week** (1 hour below federal benefits threshold of 20 hrs)

**Union Situation:**
- Starbucks Workers United represents baristas at 500+ stores
- **December 2025 strike:** "Red Cup Rebellion" — 3,800+ baristas across 180+ stores in 130+ cities; longest national work stoppage in SBUX history
- **Union wage demands:** 65% immediate pay increase + 77% over 3 years (company characterization; union disputes this as "menu of options")
- **SBUX spending:** $240M on anti-union efforts since 2021
- **Contract status:** No collective bargaining agreement reached as of March 2026

**Labor Cost Impact on P&L:**
- Labor investments are the **dominant driver** (~2/3) of the 420 bps NA margin compression
- Green Apron Service requires additional staffing hours to meet 4-minute service targets
- $2B cost savings target over 2-3 years intended to partially offset labor inflation

### 4B. Menu Simplification

- Menu reduced by **25-30%** — removed low-volume SKUs to simplify operations
- Minimized waste and supply chain complexity
- Three innovation pillars:
  1. **Health & Wellness:** Protein-based beverages
  2. **Afternoon "Reset":** Leveraging digital menu boards for daypart-specific offerings
  3. **Artisanal Bakery:** High-quality pastries to drive food attachment rates
- Food attachment rate improvement is a key P&L lever (higher-margin dayparts)

### 4C. Store Closures & Portfolio Optimization

- **627 stores closed** over 3 months (net closure: 107 after openings)
- **90%+** of closures were in North America
- Focused on **underperforming locations in crowded urban markets**: NYC, LA, Chicago, SF, Baltimore
- Part of **$1B restructuring effort**
- **Pickup-only stores being phased out** — converting to full coffeehouse format or closing
- End of FY2026: ~18,300 stores across U.S. and Canada (net growth resuming)

**Source:** [CNN Store Closures](https://www.cnn.com/2025/12/29/business/starbucks-coffee-new-york-los-angeles) | [Yahoo Finance 400 Closures](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/starbucks-closes-400-u-stores-113233255.html) | [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91432205/starbucks-store-closures-update-us-locations-list-bigger-estimates)

### 4D. Store Renovation & New Formats

- **1,000 store "uplifts"** planned by end of FY2026 — adding chairs, couches, tables, power outlets
- Cost: ~$150K per store uplift
- **25,000+ new cafe seats** across U.S. by end of FY2026
- **"Ristretto" store format**: New smaller-format prototype
  - ~20-30% lower build cost
  - 32 seats, drive-thru capability
  - Full coffeehouse experience in compact footprint
  - First pilot: converted pickup-only location in NYC
  - Designed for markets without space for full-size stores
- **5,000 new U.S. coffeehouse opportunities** identified long-term

**Source:** [NRN Store Renovations](https://www.nrn.com/quick-service/starbucks-plans-1-000-store-renovations-by-end-of-2026-and-tests-out-new-budget-prototype) | [Retail TouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/features/news-briefs/starbucks-to-complete-1000-store-uplifts-in-2026-plans-to-phase-out-pickup-only-locations)

### 4E. Staffing & Service Improvements

- **Green Apron Service:** Fully rolled out across all North America company-operated stores
  - 650 pilot stores outperformed control by **200 bps** in comp sales
  - Peak throughput now **under 4 minutes** average across cafe + drive-thru
  - **SmartQueue technology:** Double-digit improvement in cafe orders handed off under 4 minutes; **80% of in-cafe orders** now meet 4-min target
- **AI tools:**
  - **Green Dot Assist:** AI for drive-thru order-taking
  - Automated counting and supply chain scheduling
  - **Mastrena 3:** Next-gen proprietary espresso equipment
- **Supply chain:** Targeting **90% daily resupply** by end of FY2026
- Mobile Order & Pay: More accurate and on time; pickup-only format being discontinued

### 4F. Reimagined Loyalty Program (Launching March 10, 2026)

- **Three-tier structure:** Green -> Gold -> Reserve
- **Earning rates:** 1 Star/$1 (Green), 1.2 Stars/$1 (Gold), 1.7 Stars/$1 (Reserve)
- Reserve tier: 2,500 Stars over 12 months; exclusive merchandise/events
- **New 60-Star redemption tier:** $2 off any item
- Stars **never expire** at Gold and Reserve tiers
- **"Free Mod Mondays":** Free drink modification once per month for members
- **Partner integrations:** Delta SkyMiles, Marriott Bonvoy
- Current active members: **35.5M** (90-day active, record high, +3% YoY)

## 5. China JV Update (Boyu Capital)

### 5A. Deal Status

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Announcement Date** | November 3, 2025 |
| **Structure** | Boyu acquires 60% of China retail ops; SBUX retains 40% + brand IP |
| **Enterprise Value** | ~$4B (cash-free, debt-free) for 100% |
| **Boyu Payment** | ~$2.4B for 60% stake |
| **SBUX "Total Value" Claim** | >$13B (EV + retained interest + NPV licensing) |
| **Expected Close** | Q2 FY2026 (late April 2026) |
| **Regulatory Status** | Pending Chinese regulatory approval (no public update on progress) |
| **Potential Co-Investors** | Tencent Holdings, GIC (Singapore SWF) |

### 5B. Strategic Rationale

- **SBUX keeps:** Brand IP, licensing rights, Kunshan Coffee Innovation Park, Yunnan Farmer Support Center
- **SBUX sheds:** Operating risk, capex burden, local competition exposure
- **Royalty structure:** Not publicly disclosed; reference: MCD franchise royalties run 4-6% of revenue
- **Management guided:** Royalty income expected to "largely offset" lost operating income from 60% stake
- **Growth target under JV:** 8,000 stores -> **20,000 stores** (2.5x expansion)
- **International doubling:** SBUX targets ~40,000 stores outside U.S. long-term, with China 15,000-20,000

### 5C. Competitive Landscape in China

| Metric | Starbucks China | Luckin Coffee |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| **Store Count (latest)** | ~8,000 | ~29,000-30,000 |
| **Market Share** | ~14% (down from 34% in 2019) | Leading |
| **Comp Sales Q1 FY2026** | +7% (+5% tx, +2% ticket) | +13.4% (Q2 FY2025 self-op) |
| **New Store Opens (quarterly)** | ~100 net | ~2,100 (Q2 FY2025) |
| **Strategy** | Premium positioning | Aggressive price competition |

- Luckin opened its **30,000th store** in early 2026 (first high-end store in Shenzhen — direct Starbucks challenge)
- China coffee chain market intensifying with Codi Coffee, Manner Coffee, and other local players
- Starbucks' ticket-negative comp recovery in China (+9% tx but -7% ticket in Q4 FY2025) confirms **price war concession**

### 5D. Accounting & P&L Impact

- Post-close: China operations **deconsolidated** from SBUX P&L
- Revenue impact: ~$3.1B removed (FY2025 China revenue)
- Replaced by: Royalty/licensing income (estimated) + equity method income on 40% retained stake
- **FY2028 EPS guidance ($3.35-$4.00) is ex-China JV** — management noted ~$0.15 lower than if consolidated
- GAAP EPS will include a **one-time gain** on deconsolidation (fair value of retained interest vs. book value)
- Working through ASC 810 deconsolidation and ASC 606 licensing revenue recognition

**Source:** [Starbucks IR JV Announcement](https://about.starbucks.com/press/2025/starbucks-and-boyu-announce-joint-venture-for-the-next-chapter-of-growth-in-china/) | [CNBC Deal Coverage](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/03/starbucks-boyu-capital-china.html) | [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/11/04/starbucks-sells-china-unit-boyu-capital/) | [SEC 8-K Filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/829224/000082922425000079/a20251103-form8xkxex991.htm)
