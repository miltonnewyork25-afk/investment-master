## 9. Implications for SNOW Valuation

### SNOW's Relative Position:
1. **Valuation seems fair vs DDOG** -- both at ~14x TTM EV/Sales with similar ~30% growth. The question is which deserves a premium.
2. **SNOW's gross margin gap is structural** -- consumption-based cloud data platform inherently has higher COGS than pure SaaS (DDOG/MDB/ESTC). This caps long-term operating margins.
3. **The SBC elephant** -- SNOW's 34% SBC/Revenue vs peer median of ~20% means ~14pp of "excess" dilution cost. At $4.7B TTM revenue, that is ~$660M/year more SBC than a typical peer.
4. **FCF margin is misleading** -- SNOW's ~60% FCF margin looks best-in-class, but adjusting for SBC, economic margins are much lower. Owner FCF margin (FCF - SBC) is likely ~25-30%, in line with peers.
5. **Growth quality matters** -- SNOW's consumption model means revenue is more volatile (can decelerate quickly if customers optimize spend). DDOG shares this risk; MDB/ESTC/PLTR have more subscription stickiness.

### Key Questions for SNOW Analysis:
- Can gross margins expand toward 70%+ as Snowpark/AI workloads scale?
- Will SBC/Revenue normalize below 25% within 2-3 years?
- Is the consumption model a feature (land-and-expand) or a bug (volatile, optimizable)?
- How does SNOW's competitive position evolve vs Databricks (private) and cloud-native alternatives?
