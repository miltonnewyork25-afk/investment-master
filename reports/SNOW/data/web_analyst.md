## 3. Key Bull & Bear Arguments

### Bull Case

1. **AI Inflection Point**: Cortex AI, Snowflake Intelligence, and 430+ new AI features in FY2026. Over 9,100 accounts began using AI features; $100M AI revenue run rate achieved faster than expected. AI could push NRR back above 130%.
2. **Strong Revenue Growth Momentum**: Q4 FY2026 product revenue $1.227B (+30% YoY); full-year product revenue $4.472B (+29%). FY2027 guidance: $5.66B product revenue (+27%).
3. **RPO Surge**: Remaining Performance Obligations hit $9.772B in Q4 FY2026, up 42% YoY from $6.867B — signals strong forward demand visibility.
4. **Margin Expansion**: Non-GAAP operating margin expanded to 11% (from 9%); adjusted FCF margin surged to 61% (from 43%) — demonstrating operating leverage.
5. **Enterprise Fortress**: 688 customers paying $1M+; 766 Forbes Global 2000 customers. $4.4B cash for M&A optionality.
6. **TAM Expansion**: Addressable market projected to grow from $170B (data platform) to $355B (AI-driven data ecosystem) by 2029.

### Bear Case

1. **Valuation Still Stretched**: ~40x projected FY2027 FCF; ~55x when adjusting for SBC dilution. Even at $9.1B revenue by FY2028, needs 35x multiple to deliver 10% annualized return. Market cap ~$77B for a company still running GAAP losses.
2. **Massive SBC & Dilution**: $1.4-1.7B SBC in FY2026 = ~34-36% of revenue. Plus $2.3B convertible notes (due 2027/2029) adding further dilution risk. Management targets 27% SBC/Rev in FY2027, mid-teens by FY2035 — improvement pace is slow.
3. **Competition Intensifying**: Databricks at comparable $5B ARR but with 2x valuation gap (perceived AI lead). Cloud hyperscalers (Azure Fabric, BigQuery, Redshift) building competing integrated stacks. Risk of becoming a "feature within Azure" rather than standalone platform.
4. **Growth Deceleration Risk**: Bears project growth decelerating toward 20%, NRR sliding to ~120%, with Databricks taking warehousing share.
5. **Consumption Model Volatility**: Revenue tied to usage fluctuates with macro, customer optimization cycles, and seasonal patterns — harder to forecast than subscription SaaS.
6. **Bearish Target**: ~$90 (most bearish analysts), implying >45% downside.

## 6. Consumption Model: Risks & Opportunities

### Opportunities

- **Customer Flexibility**: Usage-based pricing lowers adoption barrier; customers experiment with AI features without upfront commitment, accelerating land-and-expand.
- **AI Tailwind**: AI workloads (Cortex, Snowflake Intelligence) generate incremental consumption; 9,100+ accounts using AI features = new consumption vectors.
- **NRR Mechanism**: Consumption model naturally drives NRR expansion when customers increase workloads — FY2026 NRR at ~125% (Q2 reported).
- **TAM Multiplier**: From data warehousing ($170B) to AI data ecosystem ($355B by 2029).

### Risks

- **Revenue Volatility**: Consumption fluctuates with macro conditions, optimization cycles, and seasonality — harder to model than subscription.
- **Customer Optimization Headwinds**: Enterprises actively optimizing cloud spend can reduce consumption without churning, creating "invisible" revenue pressure.
- **Forecasting Difficulty**: Wall Street struggles to model consumption accurately, leading to wider estimate ranges and price volatility.
- **Macro Sensitivity**: In downturns, consumption-based revenue gets cut faster than subscription commitments.
- **Azure/Hyperscaler Risk**: Azure is SNOW's fastest-growing cloud (+40% YoY in Q2 FY2026) but also a competitor (Fabric ecosystem) — partnership tension.

## 7. Institutional Investor Positioning

### Berkshire Hathaway Exit (Completed Q2 2024)

- **Timeline**: Invested $250M at IPO ($120/share, Sept 2020) + additional shares at debut price. Exited entire ~$990M position by Q2 2024.
- **Context**: Exit coincided with CEO change (Frank Slootman -> Sridhar Ramaswamy, Feb 2024) and wide-ranging customer data breach/cyberattack headlines.
- **Interpretation**: Buffett's exit was a vote of no-confidence in the risk/reward at that valuation, though Motley Fool and others argued investors shouldn't follow blindly given Berkshire's value-orientation mismatch with high-growth software.

### Broader Institutional Activity

- Major institutional holders remain (Vanguard, BlackRock, etc.) per standard index/ETF inclusion.
- No major new high-profile activist or concentrated fund positions reported in recent 2026 filings.
- Stock down ~20% YTD 2026 despite strong Q4 earnings — institutional sentiment appears mixed, with rotation pressure from AI hype shifting toward infrastructure plays.
