### 2.4 Vertiv's Competitive Advantages (Moat Indicators)

**1. NVIDIA Design-In Partnership (STRONGEST MOAT)** [H/S]
- Co-developed complete reference architecture for GB200 NVL72 — this becomes the de facto industry standard
- 70%+ CDU volume share on first GB200 shipments
- Once designed-in at the reference architecture level, switching costs are very high
- NVIDIA's validation and qualification process creates a moat for qualified suppliers
- **Moat type**: Design-win lock-in + Co-development ecosystem

**2. Pure-Play Data Center Focus** [H]
- Unlike Schneider (~24% DC), Eaton (partial), ABB (partial) — Vertiv is ~100% DC infrastructure
- This means 100% of R&D, sales, manufacturing capacity is aligned to DC growth
- Faster decision-making and resource allocation vs. diversified industrials
- **Moat type**: Focus advantage

**3. Global Service Network** [H]
- 300+ service centers, 4,400+ field engineers globally
- Services create recurring revenue (~18% of sales) and switching costs
- Installed base grows with each product sale — annuity-like revenue stream
- **Moat type**: Installed base + switching costs

**4. Integrated Power + Cooling Portfolio** [H]
- One of very few vendors offering both UPS/power distribution AND cooling solutions
- Enables "single throat to choke" procurement for hyperscalers
- Reduces integration risk for customers building at scale
- **Moat type**: Breadth of solution

**5. Manufacturing Scale & "Local+1" Strategy** [H]
- 24 facilities globally with active capacity expansion
- Regional manufacturing reduces lead times and tariff exposure
- Scale advantages in procurement and production efficiency
- **Moat type**: Scale + geographic reach

### 2.5 Competitive Dynamics & Trends

**Key Structural Shifts** [R/S]:

1. **AI Driving ASP Uplift**: AI racks require 10-30x more power than traditional racks. This shifts the revenue mix toward higher-ASP power and cooling products. Vertiv benefits disproportionately as a pure-play.

2. **Liquid Cooling Inflection**: Direct liquid cooling revenue more than doubled in Q1 2025 (Dell'Oro). Liquid cooling is transitioning from niche to mainstream for AI workloads. Vertiv's 70%+ CDU share gives it first-mover advantage in this $5B+ emerging market.

3. **Hyperscaler CapEx Supercycle**: Top 5 hyperscalers projected to spend ~$602B in 2026 capex [R, JLL]. Power and cooling infrastructure is a must-have for every GW deployed. Nearly 100 GW of new DC capacity to be added 2026-2030.

4. **Execution Over Plans**: Per DC Byte/JLL, the competitive differentiator in 2026 is ability to convert plans into live capacity — companies with predictable supply chains and manufacturing scale win.

5. **Schneider Convergence**: The gap between Schneider and Vertiv has narrowed from meaningful to nearly zero. Vertiv's faster growth rate suggests it may overtake Schneider in DCPI share within 2026.

6. **China De-risking**: Vertiv's "Local+1" strategy positions it well for supply chain diversification trends. Malaysia facility (Q1 2026) adds non-China APAC manufacturing.
