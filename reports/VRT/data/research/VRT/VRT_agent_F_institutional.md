1. **Broad institutional accumulation**: +24% increase in total institutional shares held — not just index rebalancing

3. **Baron Small Cap conviction**: "Valuation remains reasonable on forward estimates" — still holding large position

### Cautionary Signals
1. **Zero insider buying**: No open market purchases by any insider — no one "putting their own money in" at current prices
2. **Stock near all-time highs**: $243 vs 52-week low of $54 — some institutional buying may be momentum/index-driven, not fundamental conviction
3. **Platinum residual stake**: ~$4.4B of potential supply if they decide to exit fully
4. **Q3 2025 insider selling**: 267K shares disposed with 6 open market sales — largest quarterly insider selling in 2025

### Net Assessment
**Institutional positioning is strongly bullish.** The combination of broad-based accumulation (+24% total), high-conviction active manager buying (T. Rowe Price, Baron), reduced insider selling, and diminished Platinum overhang all point to institutional confidence in Vertiv's AI data center infrastructure growth thesis. The absence of insider buying at current prices is a moderate cautionary note but not unusual for a stock that has quintupled in 18 months.
