### Why Profitability Collapsed — Multi-Factor Breakdown

**Factor 1: Medical Cost Ratio Surge**
- MCR jumped from ~83% (2023) to ~89-89.5% (2025) — a ~600bps swing worth ~$27B in incremental medical costs
- Medicare Advantage medical cost trend came in at ~7.5% vs 5% priced expectation → $3.6B+ unpriced gap in Medicare alone
- UnitedHealthcare's additional $6.5B in medical costs vs plan, with >50% from Medicare plans

**Factor 2: Change Healthcare Cyberattack**
- February 2024 ransomware attack on Change Healthcare (acquired by UNH in 2022 for $13B)
- Total cyberattack costs: $2.3-2.45B (raised ~$1B from initial estimate)
- Impacted Q1 2024 heavily: EPS was -$1.53 in Q1 2024 (one-time charges)

**Factor 3: Elevated Operating Expenses**
- Operating expenses rose from $57.1B (FY2024) to $64.0B (FY2025) — +12.0%
- Optum Health restructuring charges, value-based care model losses
- Interest expense: $4.0B (FY2025) vs $3.9B (FY2024) — debt load from acquisitions

**Factor 4: Q4 2025 Near-Total Collapse**
- Q4 2025 operating income: **$380M** (vs $7.8B in Q4 2024) — 95% decline QoQ
- Q4 2025 net income: **$10M** (essentially breakeven)
- Q4 2025 EPS: **$0.011** (vs $5.98 in Q4 2024)
- Likely includes large one-time charges (restructuring, impairments) for Optum Health market exits

## 3. Medical Loss Ratio (MLR) Trends — Industry-Wide 2023-2025

### MLR by Market Segment

| Segment | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025E | Trend |
|---------|------|------|------|-------|-------|
| Medicare Advantage | ~86% | ~87% | ~90% | ~89-90% | Sharply up |
| Medicaid Managed Care | ~86% | ~88% | ~91% | ~90% | Decade high |
| Commercial/Group | ~83% | ~84% | ~85% | ~85% | Moderate rise |
| UNH-specific MCR | ~83% | ~84% | ~85.1% | **~89-89.5%** | Severe compression |

**Source**: [KFF Health Insurer Financial Performance in 2024](https://www.kff.org/medicare/health-insurer-financial-performance/)

### Utilization Surge Explanation

**Root Causes:**
1. **Post-COVID catch-up demand**: Deferred surgeries, screenings, and procedures from 2020-2022 being completed
2. **Medicaid redetermination unwinding**: 15M+ people lost Medicaid coverage 2023-2024; remaining population is sicker/higher-acuity
3. **Medicare Advantage star rating pressure**: CMS phasing in new risk-adjustment model (V28) reduced MA payments while utilization increased
4. **GLP-1 drug costs**: Ozempic/Wegovy/Mounjaro utilization explosion adding $2-5B+ in annual pharmacy costs across major insurers
5. **Behavioral health surge**: Mental health utilization up 20-30% post-pandemic
6. **Medicare Advantage rate cuts**: CMS rates increased only ~3.7% in 2024 vs 7-8% medical cost trend

**Industry financial impact (2024)**: Combined medical costs rose 8.9% (~$85B) while net income fell to ~$9B (0.8% profit margin) for the industry.

**Source**: [UHG Optum Health Primary Care Update](https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/content/dam/UHG/PDF/2026/2026.01-uhg-optum-health-primary-care-update.pdf), [Becker's](https://www.beckersphysicianleadership.com/news/how-optum-shifted-its-2025-strategy/)

### Strategic Shift (2025-2026)
- **Peak expansion ended**: Optum Health underwent years of aggressive M&A (acquisitions of physician groups: DaVita Medical Group, Atrius Health, Kelsey-Seybold, Agilon partnerships)
- **Now contracting**: Exiting certain markets and PPO contracts (~200K lives in 2026)
- **Refocusing on employed/dedicated physicians**: Removing less-aligned providers from networks
- **Margin crisis**: Value-based care arrangements underperformed as utilization surged — Optum couldn't manage costs effectively at scale
- **90,000 physicians** = ~10% of entire U.S. physician workforce (creating regulatory/antitrust concerns)

### Performance Claims
- UNH claims Optum Health primary care practices outperform competitors in Medicare Advantage quality metrics
- Hospital admissions for Optum Health MA patients reportedly 13% lower vs non-Optum
- But these claims are being scrutinized amid DOJ investigation into diagnosis coding practices
