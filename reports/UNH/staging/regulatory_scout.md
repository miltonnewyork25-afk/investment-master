## 1. CMS Medicare Advantage 2025-2026 Rate Changes & Star Ratings

### 2026 MA Payment Rates (Final Rate Notice, April 2025)
- **Final rate increase: +5.06%** (>$25B in total MA payments), up from +2.23% in the Advance Notice (Jan 2025)
- Effective growth rate: **9.04%** (vs 5.93% in Advance Notice) — driven by inclusion of FFS expenditure data through Q4 2024
- Advance Notice had proposed +4.33% (~$21B increase)
- **Context**: Trump administration CMS dramatically raised payments vs. Biden-era trajectory; UNH stock +6% in after-hours on announcement
- Source: [CMS 2026 MA Rate Announcement](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/2026-medicare-advantage-part-d-rate-announcement)

### Star Ratings (2026 Contract Year)
- **UNH: 77% of members in plans rated 4+ stars** for 2026, roughly flat vs 2025 — favorable outcome
- CMS increased stars for **12 UnitedHealth contracts** in revised ratings; 3 more contracts reached 4-star threshold, 2 upgraded to 5-star (maximum quality bonus rebates)
- **Litigation win**: Federal judge in Texas ordered CMS to recalculate UNH's 2025 scores (disputed call removed), potentially yielding significant increase in quality bonus payments for 2026
- Industry average: Flat after consecutive years of declines
- **Comparative**: UNH stable; Humana & Aetna declined; Elevance & Centene improved
- Source: [Healthcare Dive - Star Ratings](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/2026-medicare-advantage-star-ratings-winners-losers/802572/)

### MA Membership Contraction
- UNH expects to **lose ~1.3M Medicare Advantage members** in 2026 due to deliberate plan exits from unprofitable markets
- ~600,000 enrollees affected by plan exits; ~180,000 seniors face plan changes
- Total membership decline projection: **up to 2.8M members across all lines** in 2026
- Source: [Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/payers/unitedhealth-shares-fall-premarket-company-misses-revenue-releases-2026-outlook)

### Medical Cost Ratio Crisis (2025)
- **Full-year 2025 adjusted MCR: 88.9%** vs 85.5% in 2024 (+340bps YoY)
- Q4 2025 MCR: 92.4% (vs 92.1% consensus)
- **2025 guidance suspended** in May 2025 — first earnings miss since 2008 financial crisis
- Cause: MA beneficiaries new to UNH had medical costs **2x higher than expected**; care activity acceleration broadened beyond Q1
- **2026 outlook**: Company expects return to growth; issued 2026 guidance with Jan 2026 earnings
- Source: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/13/unitedhealth-group-ceo-andrew-witty-steps-down.html), [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/unitedhealth-unh-q4-2025-medicare-advantage-troubles/810570/)

## 2. FTC PBM Investigation — Optum Rx

### Case Background
- **Sep 20, 2024**: FTC filed administrative complaint against 3 largest PBMs — **Caremark, Express Scripts, Optum Rx**
- Focus: 51 specialty generic drugs (cancer, heart disease, HIV) dispensed 2017-2022 to commercial + Medicare Part D members
- Core allegation: PBMs engaged in practices that inflated drug costs for patients

### Current Status (as of March 2026)
- **Express Scripts settled** (Feb 4, 2026): Landmark settlement requiring fundamental business practice changes; expected to reduce patient out-of-pocket costs by up to **$7B over 10 years**
- **Optum Rx + Caremark**: Making "significant progress" in settlement talks with FTC; stay extended to advance discussions
- Administrative case against Optum Rx and Caremark **continues** — no final resolution yet
- Source: [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/optumrx-caremark-progress-ftc-settlement-insulin-case/813834/), [Goodwin Law](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/02/alerts-lifesciences-antc-express-scripts-settles-pbm-ftc-action)

### FTC Reports
- FTC released **second report** (Jan 2025) slamming PBMs for "wild profiteering and self-dealing"
- Source: [NCPA](https://ncpa.org/newsroom/news-releases/2025/01/14/new-ftc-report-pbms-shows-wild-profiteering-and-self-dealing)

### Investment Impact Assessment
- Express Scripts settlement sets **precedent** for Optum Rx terms — likely forced transparency + delinking compensation from drug prices
- Settlement risk: Structural change to Optum Rx business model (rebate pass-through, flat-fee arrangements)
- Timeline: Settlement likely in 2026 based on "significant progress" language

## 3. DOJ Antitrust — Vertical Integration Scrutiny

### Investigation Timeline
- **Feb 2024**: DOJ opened antitrust review of Optum's physician practice acquisitions
- **Oct 2023**: UNH reportedly aware of DOJ investigation (per insider trading lawsuit)
- **Nov 2025**: Renewed/intensified scrutiny reported; UNH stock dropped on Nov 10, 2025

### Scope of Investigation
1. **Optum physician group acquisitions**: Whether UNH favored Optum-owned physician groups via contracting practices, excluding rival groups
2. **Self-dealing pricing**: Health Affairs study (Nov 2025) found UNH pays Optum-owned practices **17% more** than outside practices, rising to **61% more** in markets with >25% market share
3. **Diagnosis upcoding**: Whether Optum excessively documents patient health conditions to increase Medicare payments
4. Source: [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/unitedhealth-antitrust-investigation-doj-unitedhealthcare-optum/708727/)

### Legislative Response
- **Sep 2025**: "Patients Over Profits Act" introduced — specifically targets UNH's vertical integration model
- Sponsors: Reps. Ryan (D-NY), Hoyle (D-OR), Jayapal (D-WA); Sens. Merkley (D-OR), Warren (D-MA)
- Source: [Congressman Pat Ryan](https://patryan.house.gov/media/press-releases/department-justice-investigation-unitedhealth-delayed-staff-reductions)

### DOJ Staffing Constraint
- Investigation reportedly **delayed by DOJ staff reductions** under Trump administration
- Rep. Pat Ryan demanded AG Bondi take "immediate action" — political pressure ongoing
- Source: [Congressman Pat Ryan press release](https://patryan.house.gov/media/press-releases/department-justice-investigation-unitedhealth-delayed-staff-reductions)

## 4. Change Healthcare Cyberattack Aftermath (2024-2026)

### Attack Facts
- **Date**: Feb 21, 2024 (hackers accessed Feb 12 via compromised Citrix credentials lacking MFA)
- **Perpetrator**: ALPHV/BlackCat ransomware gang
- **Ransom paid**: $22M
- **Individuals affected**: **192.7M** (as of Jul 31, 2025) — largest healthcare data breach ever recorded (~2/3 of US population)
- Source: [HIPAA Journal](https://www.hipaajournal.com/change-healthcare-responding-to-cyberattack/)

### Financial Impact
| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Total cyberattack impact (FY2024) | **$3.09B** |
| Direct response costs | $2.2B |
| Business disruption | $867M |
| Provider loans advanced | **$9.03B** (of which $4.5B repaid) |
| Direct financial support to providers | >$2B |
| Ransom payment | $22M |

- Cost progression: $870M (Q1) → $2.3-2.45B (mid-year revision) → $2.87B (Q3) → **$3.09B (FY2024 final)**
- Source: [Cybersecurity Dive](https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/unitedhealths-cyberattack-costs-23b/721579/), [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/news/change-healthcare-cyberattack-exceeds-1-billion-costs)

### Litigation Status
- **78 lawsuits** filed (individual + class action), consolidated in Minnesota (JPML, Jun 7, 2024)
- Breakdown: 19 consumer complaints (data compromise) + 30 provider complaints (claims/payment disruption) + others
- **Motions to dismiss deadline**: Mar 21, 2025; Plaintiff response: Apr 25, 2025; Defendant reply: May 23, 2025
- Settlement meetings: Dec 18, 2024 (plaintiffs), Jan 30, 2025 (defendants)
- **Nebraska AG lawsuit survived motion to dismiss** — state-level litigation expanding
- Source: [About Lawsuits](https://www.aboutlawsuits.com/change-healthcare-lawsuit/), [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/change-healthcare-cyberattack-lawsuit-consolidation/712492/)

### Provider Loan Recovery Controversy
- UNH adopted **aggressive approach** to recover outstanding loan balances from affected providers
- Providers report company is pursuing repayments despite earlier assurances of flexibility
- Some providers still struggling financially from fallout
- Source: [SAHM Capital](https://www.sahmcapital.com/news/content/unitedhealth-demands-rapid-repayment-of-cyberattack-relief-loan-billions-providers-say-push-undermines-recovery-2025-04-15)

## 5. PBM Transparency Legislation (Federal + State)

### Federal: Consolidated Appropriations Act 2026 (Signed Feb 3, 2026)
**Most significant PBM reform in US history.** Key provisions:
1. **Delink PBM compensation** in Medicare Part D from drug prices/rebate arrangements
2. **100% rebate pass-through**: PBMs must remit all rebates, fees, alternative discounts to ERISA-governed plans
3. **Detailed reporting**: PBMs must report to PDP sponsors: AWC, AWP, manufacturer rebate info, total manufacturer-derived revenue per drug
4. **Executive Order 14273**: Trump instructed DOL to propose regulations improving employer health plan transparency into PBM compensation
- Source: [Sidley Austin](https://www.sidley.com/en/insights/newsupdates/2026/02/congress-passes-significant-federal-pharmacy-benefit-manager-reform-impacting-pharmaceutical-market), [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/30/2026-01907/improving-transparency-into-pharmacy-benefit-manager-fee-disclosure)

### Federal: S.526 — PBM Transparency Act of 2025 (119th Congress)
- Additional standalone transparency legislation moving through Congress
- Source: [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/526)

### State-Level Reform (2025-2026)
- **All 50 states** have now passed some form of PBM regulation
- **Arkansas HB 1150**: First state to **ban PBM ownership of pharmacies** — enforcement paused (legal challenge)
- **California + Colorado**: Adopted PBM delinking compensation model laws (flat fees vs drug list prices)
- **Utah HB 257**: Drug rebate pass-through requirements (savings to consumers at point of sale)
- **Massachusetts + Colorado**: New licensing and PBM delinking reforms
- Source: [MultiState](https://www.multistate.us/insider/2026/1/27/pharmacy-benefit-manager-pbm-legislation-tackled-ownership-restrictions-transparency-and-more-in-2025), [NASHP](https://nashp.org/state-tracker/state-pharmacy-benefit-manager-legislation/)

### Impact on Optum Rx
- **Direct threat**: 100% rebate pass-through eliminates spread pricing profit — fundamental business model change
- **Arkansas pharmacy ownership ban** (if upheld) sets precedent that could force structural separation
- **Timeline**: CAA 2026 provisions have varying implementation dates; state laws already in effect or pending enforcement

## 7. Medicare Advantage Prior Authorization & Claim Denial Regulation

### CY 2026 Final Rule (CMS, April 4, 2025)
Key changes directly impacting UNH:

1. **Inpatient admission protection**: Plans can no longer reopen/modify a previously approved inpatient admission decision based on post-approval info — only for obvious error or fraud
2. **Prior authorization honor requirement**: Plans must honor medical necessity decisions rendered during PA process (closes loophole)
3. **Appeals process fix**: Patient liability cannot be determined until provider files a claim — prevents plans from denying appeal rights by calling it a "payment matter"
4. **Transparency mandate (2026)**: MA organizations must publish:
   - Full list of all items/services requiring PA
   - **8 distinct metrics** on PA approval/denial rates at contract level
5. **Processing timeframes (2026)**: Standard PA requests: ≤7 calendar days; Expedited: ≤72 hours
- Source: [CMS Fact Sheet](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/contract-year-2026-policy-and-technical-changes-medicare-advantage-program-medicare-prescription-final)

### Suspended Rules (June 16, 2025)
- CMS **suspended** enforcement of:
  - Health equity expertise requirement in UM committees
  - UM Committee plan-level disparity reports on approval/denial rates
- **Net effect**: Partial regulatory relief for MA plans — reduces compliance burden
- Source: [Georgetown Medicare Policy](https://medicare.chir.georgetown.edu/cms-suspends-new-medicare-advantage-prior-authorization-transparency-rules-amid-public-concerns-about-care-denials/)

### Investment Implications
- **Negative**: Transparency requirements expose denial rate data publicly — reputational + political risk for high-denial plans
- **Positive**: Suspended equity/disparity reporting reduces compliance costs
- **Structural**: PA reform limits ability to use retroactive denial as cost management tool — direct MLR pressure

## 8. Medicaid Contract Wins/Losses (2025-2026)

### Louisiana — MAJOR LOSS
- **Dec 2025**: Louisiana Department of Health announced it would **not renew** UNH Medicaid contract (expiration Dec 31, 2025)
- **330,700 enrollees affected** (one-third of Louisiana's 1.53M Medicaid population)
- Contract extended 90 days through **Mar 31, 2026** (transition period)
- **Root cause**: State litigation over PBM transparency — UNH accused of violating contract by withholding Optum Rx prescription drug pricing documents; State AG alleged UHC/Optum Rx **overcharged for prescription medications**
- Aetna also dropped in same action
- Source: [Louisiana Illuminator](https://lailluminator.com/2025/12/19/unitedhealthcare-louisiana/), [Louisiana Dept of Health](https://ldh.la.gov/news/7615)

### Aggregate Medicaid Membership Outlook
- **2025 year-end**: 7.4M Medicaid members
- **2026 projection**: Loss of **565,000-715,000 Medicaid members** (including D-SNP enrollees)
- Community & State segment revenues surged **17% to $94.4B** in 2025 despite 55K member drop — driven by rate increases for complex-needs populations
- Source: [Becker's](https://www.beckerspayer.com/financial/unitedhealthcare-projects-up-to-2-8-million-membership-decline-in-2026/)

### Strategic Context
- UNH appears to be **deliberately shedding low-margin Medicaid contracts** as part of margin recovery strategy
- State Medicaid rate inadequacy cited as ongoing issue — payments not matching member acuity
- Some rate increases received but insufficient
