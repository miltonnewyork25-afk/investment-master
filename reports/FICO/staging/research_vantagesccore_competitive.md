## 1. VantageScore Market Share by Segment

### Overall Adoption
- **42 billion** VantageScore credit scores used in 2024, up 55% from 26.9B in 2023
- **3,700+ institutions** use VantageScore, including 9 of top 10 US banks
- VantageScore's usage is heavily concentrated in **non-mortgage** segments; mortgage was essentially zero pre-July 2025

### By Segment

| Segment | VantageScore Penetration | Notes |
|---------|------------------------|-------|
| **Credit Cards** | 24.4B uses in 2024 (+142% YoY) | Largest segment by volume |
| **Auto Loans** | 34%+ growth in 2023; 42% growth in overall usage | Strong and growing |
| **Personal Loans** | Significant fintech adoption | Part of overall 3,700+ institutions |
| **Mortgage** | Near-zero before July 2025 | 74% YoY growth in H1 2025 (from tiny base) |
| **ABS Market** | $22.5B issuances in 2025 (vs $10.3B in 2022) | Growing acceptance in securitization |

### Critical Context
- The 42B "uses" figure is inflated by **screening/marketing pulls**, not just origination decisions
- FICO still dominates **decisioning** (actual lending decisions): ~90% of mortgage lenders rely on FICO
- VantageScore's strength is in **pre-qualification, marketing, and account management** — not final credit decisions
- Mortgage is the **crown jewel** — highest per-score pricing, and VantageScore had zero share until July 2025

## 2. FHFA July 2025 Announcement — What Changed

### Timeline
| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **2018** | Credit Score Competition Act signed (part of EGRRCPA) — legal foundation for multi-model scoring |
| **Oct 2022** | FHFA validates both VantageScore 4.0 and FICO 10T; announces 3-year grace period for implementation |
| **Q4 2025** | Original deadline for lender readiness |
| **July 8, 2025** | FHFA Director Bill Pulte announces **immediate** acceptance of VantageScore 4.0 — accelerating timeline |
| **Nov 2025** | Fannie Mae announces disclosure enhancements to support VantageScore 4.0 in MBS |
| **Late 2025** | FHFA nearing deal with FICO on FICO 10T implementation (still pending as of search date) |

### What Exactly Changed (July 8, 2025)
1. **VantageScore 4.0 approved for immediate use** on all conforming mortgages sold to Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac
2. **Lender choice model**: Lenders can choose between Classic FICO OR VantageScore 4.0 (not both on same loan)
3. **Classic FICO remains valid** — this is NOT a replacement, it's an addition
4. **FICO 10T NOT yet approved** — still in negotiation; only Classic FICO and VantageScore 4.0 are currently accepted
5. Director Pulte framed this as "increasing competition" and "lowering costs" consistent with Trump administration mandate

### Stock Impact
- FICO shares **fell 19%** on the day of announcement (to ~$1,522.61 intraday)
- This was the single biggest regulatory threat to FICO's pricing power in decades

### Key Nuance: FICO 10T Status
- FICO 10T was validated alongside VantageScore 4.0 in 2022
- But FICO 10T implementation is **still pending** — FHFA and FICO are "nearing a deal" as of Nov 2025
- This creates an asymmetry: VantageScore 4.0 is live, FICO 10T is not yet
- FICO launched "Mortgage Direct" program (Oct 2025) as defensive response

## 3. Pricing Comparison

### Per-Score Costs (Mortgage Context)

| Model | Price per Score | Source | Notes |
|-------|----------------|--------|-------|
| **Classic FICO (traditional)** | $10.00 | FICO blog | Traditional per-score model |
| **Classic FICO (new performance-based)** | $4.95 royalty + $33 funded loan fee | FICO blog | Launched Oct 2025 via "Mortgage Direct" |
| **FICO wholesale royalty** | $3.50 → $4.95 (2025) | HousingWire | 41% increase in 2025 |
| **VantageScore 4.0 (Equifax)** | $4.50 | Equifax press release | Fixed through 2027; "50%+ below FICO" |
| **VantageScore 4.0 (Experian)** | **FREE** | Experian press release | Free indefinitely to mortgage clients |
| **VantageScore 4.0 (TransUnion)** | $4.00 (2026) | National Mortgage News | Free bundled with FICO purchase through end of 2026 |

### Tri-Merge Credit Report (Full Package) Costs
- Current range: **$80-$140** per tri-merge report (includes FICO scores)
- MBA projects costs could increase **40-50%** in 2026
- VantageScore option could reduce total report costs by $15-30 per pull

### Investment Implications
- Credit bureaus are **dumping VantageScore at/below cost** to gain share — classic platform economics
- FICO's $4.95 "Mortgage Direct" pricing is a **defensive price cut** that bypasses bureaus — effectively "declaring war on its own distribution partners"
- The bureaus collectively represent **51% of FICO's total revenue** (FY2025, up from 45% in FY2024) — this is a dangerous conflict of interest

## 4. Credit Bureau Incentives to Promote VantageScore

### Ownership Structure
- VantageScore Solutions, LLC — **jointly owned** by Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion (formed 2006)
- This creates a fundamental conflict: the three entities that distribute FICO scores also own FICO's competitor

### Bureau-Specific Incentive Programs (Post-July 2025)

| Bureau | VantageScore Pricing | Bundle Strategy | Strategic Intent |
|--------|---------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Equifax** | $4.50 fixed through 2027 | Free VantageScore with any FICO purchase (mortgage/auto/card/consumer finance) | Most aggressive; explicit "50% below FICO" messaging |
| **Experian** | **Free indefinitely** | Free to all mortgage clients | Most radical; pure share-grab |
| **TransUnion** | $4.00 in 2026 | Free VantageScore with FICO purchase through end of 2026 | Following Equifax/Experian playbook |

### Strategic Logic for Bureaus
1. **Revenue capture**: Every VantageScore that replaces a FICO score = bureau keeps the royalty instead of paying FICO
2. **Vertical integration**: Bureaus own data + own scoring model = higher margins
3. **Negotiating leverage**: Even if VantageScore doesn't win outright, its existence forces FICO to lower prices
4. **FICO concentration risk**: Bureaus collectively pay 51% of FICO's revenue — they have enormous leverage
5. **FICO "Mortgage Direct" threat**: FICO's October 2025 move to sell directly to lenders (bypassing bureau markup) gave bureaus **additional** motivation to promote VantageScore

### The Structural Conflict
- FICO relies on bureaus for distribution (51% of revenue)
- Bureaus own FICO's competitor
- FICO responded by trying to go direct-to-lender (Mortgage Direct)
- This further antagonized bureaus, creating a negative spiral
- **Key question for FICO investors**: Can FICO maintain pricing power when its distribution partners are actively subsidizing the competitor?

## 5. FICO 10T vs VantageScore 4.0 — Technical Accuracy Claims

### FICO's Claims (Source: FICO white paper, July 2025)
- FICO 10T's improvement over Classic FICO is **5x better** than VantageScore 4.0's improvement
- FICO 10T detects **18% more defaulters** in the critical score decile used for mortgage origination
- VantageScore 4.0 detects only **3.4% more defaulters** vs Classic FICO in the same decile
- Analysis conducted by "independent third party" (commissioned by FICO)

### VantageScore's Counterpoints
- Called FICO 10T "secret vaporware" — arguing it's not yet commercially available at scale
- Claims "multiple industry participants have privately confirmed VantageScore 4.0 is more predictive than FICO 10T" based on real-world loan data
- Bank of America analysts independently found VantageScore 4.0 to be "the most predictive credit score for mortgages"
- VantageScore 4.0 incorporates alternative data (rent, utility, telecom payments)

### Fairness Dimension
- **VantageScore criticism**: Uses mortgage-specific variables that may penalize people who have never owned a home (young people, military, disadvantaged groups)
- **FICO 10T claim**: Includes rental data WITHOUT penalizing non-homeowners
- This is a significant regulatory/political dimension — financial inclusion is a key policy objective

### Assessment for Investment Analysis
- Both sides cite studies favorable to their product — **neither is independently verified by a neutral party**
- The accuracy difference is likely **marginal** — the real competition is on price, distribution, and regulatory access
- VantageScore's advantage: it's **live now** in the GSE market; FICO 10T is still pending
- FICO 10T's advantage: if approved, it may offer genuinely better predictive power and fairness characteristics

## 6. Lender Adoption Data

### VantageScore Adoption
- **3,700+ institutions** use VantageScore (across all products, not just mortgage)
- **9 of top 10 US banks** use VantageScore in some capacity (likely screening/marketing, not primary decisioning)
- H1 2025 online mortgage score usage: **+74% YoY** (from very small base)
- BMO publicly committed to VantageScore 4.0 for mortgage lending

### FICO Adoption
- **~90% of mortgage lenders** rely on FICO for origination decisions
- **40+ mortgage lenders** adopted FICO 10T as of Feb 2026 — mostly community lenders for non-conforming mortgages
- FICO 10T early adopters represent **$300B in annual originations** and **$1.5T in servicing portfolios**

### Key Observation
- VantageScore's "3,700 institutions" figure is misleading for mortgage analysis — most usage is non-mortgage
- In mortgage specifically, FICO remains dominant for actual origination decisions
- The July 2025 FHFA decision is the **opening of the door**, not evidence of mass adoption
- Real adoption data (what % of new conforming loans use VantageScore vs Classic FICO) is not yet publicly available

## 8. International Credit Scoring Alternatives

### FICO's Global Position
- FICO operates in **100+ countries** with localized scoring models
- US market is FICO's revenue core; international is relatively small
- Jan 2026: FICO partnered with Plaid on next-gen UltraFICO Score (US-focused)

### Regional Alternatives

| Region | Dominant Scoring | Key Players | Notes |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|-------|
| **US** | FICO (~90% mortgage) | FICO, VantageScore | Duopoly emerging |
| **UK** | Bureau-specific scores | Experian, Equifax, TransUnion | No single dominant model like FICO |
| **EU** | Bureau-specific + bank internal | CRIF, Schufa (Germany), Banque de France | Fragmented by country |
| **China** | Zhima Credit (Ant Group) + PBOC | Ant Group, Baidu, Tencent | Alternative data-heavy; regulatory tightening |
| **India** | CIBIL (TransUnion) | CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF | Growing rapidly with digital lending |
| **Brazil** | Serasa (Experian) + SPC | Experian-owned Serasa dominates | Positive credit registry launched 2019 |
| **Australia** | Comprehensive Credit Reporting | Equifax, Experian, illion | Mandatory CCR since 2018 |

### Market Size
- Global credit scoring market: **$17.47B** (2024), projected **$55.64B** by 2032 (CAGR 15.58%)
- Alternative credit scoring market: **$1.5B** (2025), projected **$11.7B** by 2035 (CAGR 23.10%)
- North America holds **56.8%** of alternative data market (2024)
- EU and Asia-Pacific: only **40-60% adult credit file coverage** (vs 90%+ in US)

### Cross-Border Development
- March 2025: Equifax launched **Real-Time Global Credit Network** — cross-border data sharing between US, UK, Australia for expat borrowers
- 67% of lenders globally now use some form of alternative data

### Investment Implication
- FICO's international moat is **weaker** than domestic — no regulatory mandate equivalent to US mortgage market
- VantageScore has **no meaningful international presence** — this is purely a US fight
- The real international threat is from **bureau-owned proprietary scores** and **fintech alternative scoring**

## Investment-Relevant Summary

### Bear Case for FICO (VantageScore Threat)
1. **Price compression inevitable**: Bureaus dumping VantageScore at $0-$4.50 vs FICO's $4.95-$10
2. **Distribution conflict**: 51% of FICO revenue comes from entities that own the competitor
3. **Regulatory tailwind for VantageScore**: FHFA explicitly promoting "competition" and "lower costs"
4. **First-mover advantage**: VantageScore 4.0 is live in GSE market; FICO 10T still pending
5. **Financial inclusion narrative**: VantageScore scores 33M+ additional consumers — powerful political support

### Bull Case for FICO (Defensibility)
1. **Mortgage is only one segment**: Scores revenue is diversified across auto, card, personal, insurance
2. **FICO 10T technical superiority claims**: If validated, may win on merit
3. **Switching costs**: Lender risk models, compliance frameworks, and secondary market practices are built around FICO
4. **Track record**: 30+ years of performance data; VantageScore has limited mortgage track record
5. **"Mortgage Direct" option**: Can compete on price by going direct ($4.95 vs bureau-distributed $10)
6. **Non-mortgage segments unaffected**: Auto, card, insurance still heavily FICO-dependent
7. **International opportunity**: VantageScore is US-only; FICO has global footprint

### Key Numbers for FICO Financial Model
- Scores segment: **88% operating margins**, **27% revenue growth** (FY2025)
- B2B Scores: **$948.6M** (81% of segment revenue)
- Mortgage = **55% of B2B revenue** — this is the segment under attack
- Bureau concentration: **51% of total revenue** from Equifax/Experian/TransUnion
- Stock dropped **19%** on July 8, 2025 announcement
