## CQ1. What is FORM really being paid for by the market today?

### Answer

FORM is primarily being paid for a future quality transition, not for trailing economics.

Why:

- current valuation is rich
- current `ROIC` and `FCF` do not yet look like those of a fully proven high-quality test compounder
- even full target-model execution only makes today's price look reasonable if the market continues to grant a premium quality multiple

### Current confidence

`High`

---

## CQ2. Is the current growth wave broad, or mostly HBM / DRAM concentrated?

### Answer

The growth wave is real, but current evidence suggests it is narrower and more memory-heavy than the market may assume.

Why:

- memory mix expanded through 2025
- `SK hynix` stayed near one-fifth to one-quarter of revenue for much of the year
- broad company-level acceleration only became obvious in Q4 rather than throughout the entire year

### Current confidence

`Medium-High`

---

## CQ3. Can FORM convert demand strength into durable margins and free cash flow?

### Answer

Yes, plausibly, but not yet conclusively.

Why:

- the path to a better operating model is credible
- but the bridge is still burdened by expansion cost, capex intensity, and the need for sustained demand duration
- even after normalizing some `Farmers Branch` distortion, the current economics still sit far below the target model

### Current confidence

`Medium`

---

## CQ4. Is the balance sheet a strength, or does capital intensity still constrain the story?

### Answer

The balance sheet is a strength, but capital intensity still constrains the equity story.

Why:

- solvency and liquidity are solid
- but the issue is not balance-sheet fragility
- the issue is how much real cash must still be spent before the business reaches the cleaner steady state implied by the target model

### Current confidence

`High`

---

## CQ5. Does signaling weaken the clean bull case?

### Answer

Yes, modestly.

Why:

- `100baggers` summary flags insider net selling
- the repo's direct insider endpoint is weak, so this is not yet a fully closed question
- more importantly, management's target model can be misunderstood if investors compress the bridge and the destination into the same thing

### Current confidence

`Medium`

---

## CQ6. What peer set actually explains the correct multiple?

### Answer

No single peer set is perfect, which is itself an important conclusion.

Better framework:

- economically, FORM is closer to a probe-card-led, test-intensity business
- strategically, it aspires to broader platform relevance
- valuation-wise, the market is already borrowing language from stronger, higher-quality semi-cap peers

This mismatch is the core issue.

### Current confidence

`High`

---

## CQ7. What could break the current thesis in either direction over the next 4 to 6 quarters?

### Answer

The thesis will be decided less by abstract AI enthusiasm and more by a small set of practical checkpoints:

- whether revenue stays at the new higher range
- whether gross margin holds in the mid-40s
- whether memory concentration broadens or narrows further
- whether `Farmers Branch` costs and timing stay under control

### Current confidence

`High`

---

## Overall CQ Synthesis

Taken together, the CQs point to one integrated answer:

> FORM is a real and strategically relevant advanced-test company, but the stock already assumes that narrow demand strength can be converted into broad, durable, premium-quality economics. That conversion may happen, but the current valuation leaves limited room for a slower or messier path.
