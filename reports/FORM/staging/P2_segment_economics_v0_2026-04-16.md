# FORM P2 Segment Economics v0

> Date: 2026-04-16
> Purpose: determine whether FORM's internal business mix supports a broad platform valuation or a narrower probe-card-led transition story

Primary source base:

- FY2025 `10-K`
- Q4 2025 supplemental materials
- repo live financial data

---

## 1. Segment Structure

FORM reports two operating segments:

- `Probe Cards`
- `Systems`

This matters because the company can sound more diversified than it economically is.

The hard segment reality is simpler:

- `Probe Cards` is the main business
- `Systems` is important, but still secondary

---

## 2. Revenue Mix By Segment

Official revenue by segment:

| Year | Probe Cards ($M) | Systems ($M) | Total ($M) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023 | 497.9 | 165.2 | 663.1 |
| 2024 | 626.0 | 137.6 | 763.6 |
| 2025 | 637.9 | 147.1 | 785.0 |

What this says:

- `Probe Cards` was about `81%` of total FY2025 revenue
- `Systems` was only about `19%`

Interpretation:

- FORM is still overwhelmingly a probe-card company
- the systems story may help strategy and optionality, but it does not yet change the economic center of gravity

---

## 3. Probe Cards: The Real Core

FY2025 probe-card end-market mix:

- Foundry & Logic: `$369.9M`
- DRAM: `$247.4M`
- Flash: `$20.6M`

Important internal shift:

- DRAM rose from `29.8%` of total company revenue in FY2024 to `31.6%` in FY2025
- within probe cards specifically, the 10-K says DRAM rose from `36.3%` of Probe Card sales to `38.8%`
- the same filing says Foundry & Logic fell from `60.9%` to `58.0%` of Probe Card sales

This is a very important clue.

It means the company did not just grow.
It tilted more toward DRAM.

And management explicitly says:

- DRAM products generally have lower margins than Foundry & Logic products, even if there is customer/device variation

Interpretation:

- probe-card revenue growth is not automatically margin-accretive
- a more DRAM-heavy mix can make the company look stronger top-line while still complicating the profitability story

That is one of the key hidden tensions in the entire FORM thesis.

---

## 4. Systems: Strategic Value, But Not Yet A Re-Rating Engine

The systems segment includes:

- probe stations
- thermal systems
- cryogenic systems

It is strategically interesting because it reaches into:

- lab-to-fab workflows
- specialized thermal test
- cryogenic and quantum-related applications
- co-packaged optics transition opportunities

This gives the segment real optionality.

But the economics are mixed.

### 4.1 What improved

- systems revenue rose from `$137.6M` in FY2024 to `$147.1M` in FY2025
- the increase was driven by thermal systems, probe stations, and cryogenic systems

### 4.2 What weakened

- systems gross margin fell from `51.3%` in FY2023 to `43.2%` in FY2024 to `41.8%` in FY2025
- the 10-K attributes the FY2025 decline to:
  - increased manufacturing spending
  - unfavorable product mix
  - a greater percentage of lower-margin products

Interpretation:

- systems is not currently behaving like a hidden software-like margin engine
- it is strategically useful, but economically still mixed

This is crucial for valuation language:

- investors may hear "broader platform"
- but the segment data still says "helpful second leg, not yet a premium-quality engine"

## 5. Operating Structure At The Company Level

Live multi-year operating structure:

| Year | Revenue ($M) | Gross Margin | Op Margin | EBITDA ($M) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2020 | 693.6 | 41.5% | 12.1% | 134.7 |
| 2021 | 769.7 | 41.9% | 12.7% | 143.6 |
| 2022 | 747.9 | 39.6% | 7.3% | 96.5 |
| 2023 | 663.1 | 39.0% | 12.5% | 127.1 |
| 2024 | 763.6 | 40.3% | 8.5% | 112.7 |
| 2025 | 785.0 | 39.5% | 8.5% | 105.0 |

What stands out:

- revenue reached a new high in 2025
- gross margin did not
- operating margin stayed stuck at `8.5%`
- EBITDA declined versus prior better-margin years

Interpretation:

- FORM's recent revenue progress has not yet translated into a clearly superior company-level earnings model
- that is the strongest argument against treating current scale as if it already proved future quality

---

## 6. Net Contribution Clue

The segment note gives another subtle but useful signal.

In FY2024:

- Probe Cards net contribution: `$128.3M`
- Systems net contribution: `$15.6M`

In FY2023:

- Probe Cards net contribution: `$69.4M`
- Systems net contribution: `$36.9M`

Interpretation:

- the systems segment can contribute meaningfully
- but its contribution is not stable enough to support a simple "Systems will rescue the mix" narrative

That makes the company more reliant on Probe Cards staying healthy than a casual read might suggest.

---

## 7. Segment-Economics Conclusion

The most accurate economic summary is:

> FORM is still fundamentally a probe-card-led company whose recent mix has become more memory-heavy, while the systems segment adds strategic breadth but has not yet earned the right to transform the valuation language on its own.

This matters because it sharpens the main thesis in three ways:

1. The bull case still depends mostly on Probe Cards.
2. The mix shift toward DRAM/HBM may help demand but not necessarily maximize margins.
3. The systems segment helps the story, but not enough yet to justify treating FORM like a broad, premium-quality multi-engine platform.
