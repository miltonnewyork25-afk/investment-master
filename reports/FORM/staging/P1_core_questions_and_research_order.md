# FORM P1 Core Questions And Research Order

> Date: 2026-04-16
> Purpose: convert the first-pass discovery and live data validation into a concrete deep-research sequence for the Tier 3 report

---

## Working Objective

The report should determine whether FormFactor is:

- a broad AI-era test-intensity compounder
- a narrower HBM / advanced-memory scarcity beneficiary
- or a margin-transition story that the market is already valuing as if the hard part is finished

---

## Core Questions

### CQ1. What is FORM really being paid for by the market today?

This is the valuation-language question.

Needed proof:

- compare current valuation against trailing profitability and free-cash-flow reality
- compare valuation language against management's own target model
- determine whether investors are underwriting revenue breadth, margin normalization, or both

Priority sources:

- `FMP`: profile, quote, key metrics, ratios, DCF, estimates
- `100baggers`: quarterly summary, strategy report
- official investor presentation

### CQ2. Is the current growth wave broad, or mostly HBM / DRAM concentrated?

This is the cycle-width question.

Needed proof:

- break down revenue by foundry and logic, DRAM, flash, probe cards, and systems
- map which customers and end-markets likely explain the recent acceleration
- test whether "AI test intensity" is broader than a small number of memory and networking programs

Priority sources:

- official quarterly materials
- 10-K and 10-Q segment/customer disclosures
- local relation graph and peer map

### CQ3. Can FORM actually convert demand strength into durable margins and free cash flow?

This is the operating-conversion question.

Needed proof:

- trace gross-margin bridge, opex leverage, and capex burden
- evaluate Farmers Branch start-up drag versus long-run absorption benefits
- check whether free-cash-flow improvement is operational or mostly a timing story

Priority sources:

- `FMP`: income, cash flow, key metrics
- `100baggers`: summary diagnostics
- official 10-K and 10-Q cash-flow detail

### CQ4. Is the balance sheet a strength, or does capital intensity still constrain the equity story?

This is the capital-structure question.

Needed proof:

- examine liabilities, liquidity, and capex intensity
- test whether strategic investments like `FICT` and acquisitions like `Keystone` improve optionality or dilute near-term returns
- evaluate whether the company is asset-light in narrative but asset-heavy in economics

Priority sources:

- `FMP`: balance, cash flow, financial scores
- official filings

### CQ5. Does insider behavior or management signaling weaken the clean bull case?

This is the signal-integrity question.

Needed proof:

- inspect insider selling context
- check if management messaging is ahead of economic proof
- distinguish ordinary compensation-related selling from thesis-relevant distribution

Priority sources:

- `100baggers`: summary signal layer
- `FMP`: insider-trading
- official SEC filings / proxy materials where needed

### CQ6. What peer set actually explains the correct multiple?

This is the comp-framework question.

Needed proof:

- determine whether FORM should be compared mainly with probe-card peers, broader test names, semi-cap names, or mixed industrial technology names
- separate "technology relevance" from "economic similarity"
- identify which peer framing best matches margins, cycles, and capital intensity

Priority sources:

- local repo peer logic
- benchmark reports: `LRCX`, `LITE`, `COHR`
- official company descriptions and segment economics

### CQ7. What could break the current thesis in either direction over the next 4 to 6 quarters?

This is the catalyst-map question.

Needed proof:

- identify disconfirming evidence for both bull and bear narratives
- define leading indicators for memory mix, utilization, margin flow-through, and customer concentration
- turn the thesis into concrete monitoring checkpoints

Priority sources:

- official quarterly materials and filings
- `FMP` trailing and estimate series
- `100baggers` filings and summary updates

---

## Research Order

### Step 1. Build the economic base table

Collect:

- segment mix
- end-market mix
- customer concentration
- trailing valuation and quality
- cash-flow conversion

Output:

- one source-of-truth fact table for the report

### Step 2. Build the contradiction map

Translate the fact table into 3 to 4 major debate axes:

- narrative breadth vs actual concentration
- valuation vs realized economics
- margin promise vs factory-ramp friction
- scarcity narrative vs capital intensity

Output:

- final contradiction tree and report spine

### Step 3. Build the peer and multiple framework

Decide what FORM is most comparable to economically, not just technologically.

Output:

- comp table
- multiple-language decision

### Step 4. Build the operating bridge

Show how revenue mix, utilization, gross margin, opex, capex, and FCF connect.

Output:

- margin / FCF bridge
- scenario hooks

### Step 5. Build the catalyst and falsification layer

Convert the thesis into observable proof points.

Output:

- bull / bear / falsification checklist
- quarter-by-quarter monitoring grid

## Current Working Judgment

The report should currently assume:

> FORM is most likely to become a report about whether a real but narrow test-intensity surge can be turned into a durable margin-and-cash-flow re-rating before capital intensity, concentration, and execution drag reduce the market's enthusiasm.

That is a better working center of gravity than a generic "AI infrastructure winner" frame.
