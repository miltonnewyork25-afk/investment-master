## 2. Positive Catalyst Conditions

### C1. Revenue sustains above the new level

Signal:

- quarterly revenue holds around or above the `$220M+` range rather than slipping back toward the prior base

Why it matters:

- would suggest the demand step-up is more durable than a single-quarter burst

### C2. Non-GAAP gross margin remains in the mid-40s or better

Signal:

- Q1 and follow-on quarters hold around the guided `45% +/- 1.5%` non-GAAP gross-margin band

Why it matters:

- confirms that the company is actually beginning the transition toward the target model

### C3. Memory strength broadens into a broader HPC / advanced-packaging story

Signal:

- Foundry & Logic stays healthy while memory remains strong

Why it matters:

- would weaken the "too narrow" critique

### C4. Analyst Day clarifies a credible bridge, not just a destination

Signal:

- management provides concrete milestones linking output, margin, capex, and timing

Why it matters:

- helps distinguish a serious operating bridge from aspirational framing

## 3. Kill Switch Register

### KS-01: Revenue Step-Up Fails

- Trigger: revenue falls materially below the new operating range for two consecutive quarters
- Why it matters: would suggest the Q4/Q1 strength was narrower and shorter-lived than bulls assume

### KS-02: Margin Bridge Stalls

- Trigger: non-GAAP gross margin falls back decisively below the mid-40s after the Q1'26 guide period
- Why it matters: breaks the easiest version of the conversion story

### KS-03: Expansion Costs Drift Up

- Trigger: 2026 capex exceeds the official `140M-170M` range or start-up costs materially exceed `20M-25M`
- Why it matters: makes the target-model bridge slower, more expensive, and less trustworthy

### KS-04: Memory Concentration Reverses Into Weakness

- Trigger: DRAM revenue weakens for multiple quarters without offsetting broad-based strength elsewhere
- Why it matters: exposes how narrow the current demand engine really is

### KS-05: Customer Concentration Shock

- Trigger: one large customer program rolls off or visibility materially deteriorates without replacement
- Why it matters: concentration is manageable until it suddenly becomes the whole story

### KS-06: FCF Does Not Recover Despite Strong Demand

- Trigger: revenue remains healthy but free cash flow stays depressed well into the expansion period
- Why it matters: would imply the market is paying for an operating model that is not materializing on schedule

---

## 4. What To Watch Every Quarter

Use this quarterly checklist:

1. Revenue versus the new `~220M` level
2. Non-GAAP gross margin versus `45%` zone
3. Foundry & Logic mix versus DRAM mix
4. Customer concentration above `10%` thresholds
5. Capex, start-up cost, and free-cash-flow bridge updates
6. Analyst Day / earnings-call language about the timing of `Farmers Branch` benefits

---

## 5. Current Monitoring Judgment

The thesis is not currently fragile because the business is obviously bad.

It is fragile because:

- the demand may be narrower than the stock price assumes
- the conversion path may be longer than the stock price assumes
- and the stock is already priced as if those two uncertainties are mostly solved

That makes the catalyst map unusually important for FORM.
