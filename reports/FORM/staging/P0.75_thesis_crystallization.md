> Date: 2026-04-16
> Status: first-pass discovery draft
> Goal: identify the initial contradiction set before full Tier 3 buildout

## One-Sentence Core Contradiction

FORM is likely being rewarded as an AI / HBM scarcity enabler, but the company's own operating bridge looks more like a modest top-line grower trying to convert a temporary test-intensity surge into a durable margin and free-cash-flow re-rating.

---

## Constraint Collisions

### Collision 1: AI scarcity narrative vs target-model arithmetic

- Bull case language: probe intensity rises structurally with advanced packaging and HBM
- Constraint: management target model implies modest revenue growth, with the real upside coming from margin and FCF conversion

### Collision 2: secular growth framing vs cyclical customer concentration

- Bull case language: FORM is a broad semiconductor test beneficiary
- Constraint: near-term results appear strongly exposed to memory/HBM programs and a small number of large customers

### Collision 3: operating leverage promise vs capacity-ramp reality

- Bull case language: scale drives large margin expansion
- Constraint: 2026 includes meaningful factory start-up costs, capex burden, and qualification risk

---

## Initial Hypotheses

### H1

FORM's multiple expansion may be running ahead of the company's true earnings-conversion timeline.

### H2

The stock may be pricing broad AI platform relevance, while the income statement is still being driven by a narrower advanced-memory test cycle.

### H3

The key risk is not only end-market slowdown; it is that management succeeds operationally, but not fast enough to justify the premium multiple now embedded in the stock.

---

## Provisional Main Question

Is FORM a durable test-intensity compounder, or a temporarily scarce HBM test lever that the market is already pricing as if the target margin model has been achieved?
