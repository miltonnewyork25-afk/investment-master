# FORM Customer Concentration And Demand Narrowness v0

> Date: 2026-04-16
> Purpose: determine whether FormFactor's current acceleration is broad-based or concentrated in a narrow set of customers and end-markets

Primary source base:

- FY2025 `10-K`
- Q4 2025 supplemental materials
- repo live data stack

---

## 1. The Hard Customer-Concentration Facts

The most important disclosed fact:

- `SK hynix` represented `22.9%` of FY2025 revenue

Quarterly disclosed customers above the `10%` threshold during 2025:

- `2025 Q1`:
  - `SK hynix`: `23.3%`
  - `Intel`: `10.5%`
- `2025 Q2`:
  - `SK hynix`: `25.0%`
  - `Intel`: `12.4%`
  - `TSMC`: `10.4%`
- `2025 Q3`:
  - `SK hynix`: `24.5%`
  - `TSMC`: `12.0%`
- `2025 Q4`:
  - `SK hynix`: `19.2%`

What this means:

- FORM is not a one-customer company
- but it is also not broadly diffuse in the way a casual "AI picks-and-shovels" label may imply
- a small set of customers and programs matter disproportionately

---

## 2. The End-Market Narrowing Pattern

Using official quarterly market mix data:

| Quarter | Foundry & Logic | DRAM | Flash | Total Memory | SK hynix |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2025 Q1 | 49.8% | 28.5% | 1.4% | 29.9% | 23.3% |
| 2025 Q2 | 50.8% | 29.2% | 2.8% | 32.0% | 25.0% |
| 2025 Q3 | 45.8% | 33.6% | 2.6% | 36.3% | 24.5% |
| 2025 Q4 | 42.8% | 34.1% | 3.4% | 37.5% | 19.2% |

Two important shifts stand out:

1. `Foundry & Logic` remained the largest market, but its lead narrowed materially.
2. `DRAM + Flash` grew from about `29.9%` of revenue in `Q1` to about `37.5%` in `Q4`.

Interpretation:

- FORM's current acceleration increasingly leans on memory intensity, not just broad semiconductor activity
- the market may be hearing a "broad AI test intensity" story, while the actual 2025 pattern looks more like a concentrated advanced-memory and selective foundry/networking story

---

## 3. Why This Matters For The HBM Debate

`HBM` is not disclosed as a standalone segment. That matters.

The market can easily compress the story into:

- AI demand up
- HBM demand up
- probe cards more important
- FORM wins broadly

But the disclosed evidence supports a narrower reading:

- memory share rose steadily through 2025
- `SK hynix` stayed near or above one-fifth of revenue all year
- peak strength came alongside DRAM mix expansion rather than broad-based company-wide acceleration all year

Working judgment:

> FORM likely has real HBM / advanced-memory leverage, but the current evidence does not yet justify treating that leverage as if it were fully diversified across customers, programs, and cycle phases.

---

## 4. Why Customer Concentration Is Not Automatically A Bear Case

Customer concentration here is not mainly a credit-risk story.

These are elite semiconductor customers:

- `SK hynix`
- `TSMC`
- `Intel`

So the real risk is not default. The real risk is:

- program concentration
- spending normalization
- delayed qualification
- mix reversal if HBM intensity cools or broad foundry/logic does not keep pace

This makes FORM's concentration risk more subtle:

- less about catastrophic loss
- more about whether the market is over-extrapolating a narrow demand surge

---

## 5. The Right Question For The Main Report

The wrong question:

- "Is FORM exposed to AI?"

The right question:

- "How broad is FORM's AI-linked demand really, and how much of today's valuation depends on a narrow HBM / memory-heavy demand pattern staying stronger for longer?"

Current answer:

> The evidence so far suggests that FORM's demand is real but narrower than the market may be assuming. That does not break the bull case, but it does make the valuation more fragile than a broad-based AI infrastructure story would imply.
