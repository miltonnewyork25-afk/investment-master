## 12. Key Observations for Analysis

1. **Valuation extreme**: Stock up ~407% in 1 year. Trailing P/E 185x, EV/EBITDA ~94x, EV/Sales ~12.6x. Forward P/E 57x implies market expects massive earnings ramp.

2. **DRAM/HBM is the story**: DRAM segment doubled from $114M to $247M in 2 years (FY2023→FY2025). This is HBM probe card demand driven by AI/data center. DRAM went from 17% to 32% of revenue.

3. **Foundry & Logic declining share**: Still largest at 47% of revenue but shrinking from 57%. Absolute dollars down from $436M (FY2021) to $370M (FY2025).

4. **Massive CapEx investment**: FY2025 CapEx surged to $104M (from $38M prior year), crushing FCF to $12M. This signals major capacity buildout — likely for HBM probe card manufacturing.

5. **Margins are middling**: Gross margins 39-42%, operating margins 7-13%. Not a high-margin business despite semiconductor exposure. SBC is ~5% of revenue.

6. **Net cash, no dividends**: $58M net cash position. Started buybacks in FY2024 ($53M). No dividends.

7. **Quarterly trajectory improving**: Q4'25 showed best quarterly results — $215M revenue, 42.8% GM, 13.1% OPM. Sequential improvement throughout FY2025.

8. **ROIC is low**: 5-9% range. For a company trading at 12.6x sales, ROIC needs to improve dramatically to justify the valuation.
