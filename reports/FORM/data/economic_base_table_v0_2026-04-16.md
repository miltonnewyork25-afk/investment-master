## 5. What The Base Table Already Suggests

Three conclusions stand out:

1. `FORM` is not currently priced like a cyclical low-quality laggard.
2. `FORM` is also not currently delivering the trailing ROIC or FCF profile of a fully proven high-quality test compounder.
3. Therefore the market is probably paying in advance for a margin-and-cash-flow transition that still needs to be earned.

Working implication:

> The real valuation question is not whether FORM is "good" or "AI exposed." The real question is whether the stock is already pricing in a future operating model that has only begun to show itself in the numbers.
