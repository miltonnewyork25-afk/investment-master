### B) Bull Arguments
1. Hyperscaler capex growing 36% YoY to $602B — demand engine shows no signs of slowing
2. Semiconductor industry revenue on track for $975B in 2026, $1T potential — equipment is picks-and-shovels play
3. Semi Systems GM at 54.5% shows strong pricing power at the product level — overall margin drag is from mix/corporate
4. Memory capex ($61B DRAM + $21B NAND = $82B) growing faster than logic — favors AMAT's deposition/etch strength
5. "Giga-cycle" thesis: AI creates structural demand floor, not traditional cyclical peak
6. 300mm fab equipment investment surpassing $100B first time in 2025, climbing to ~$140B by 2028

### C) Bear Arguments
1. **Hyperscaler FCF crisis**: Amazon going negative FCF (-$17B to -$28B), massive debt issuance — unsustainable capex acceleration could reverse sharply
2. **Depreciation math doesn't work**: $400B annual AI asset depreciation vs combined hyperscaler profits = ROI pressure that will eventually force capex rationalization
3. **Capex growth decelerating**: 16.3% (2025) → 11.7% (2026) → potential pause (2028 per Gartner) — equipment stocks price in peak growth
4. **Memory price spike risk**: 4x consumer memory price increase → demand destruction → memory capex cuts in 2027
5. **AMAT's overall OPM (30%) and FCF margin (14.8%) are inferior to peers** — less operational leverage when cycle turns
6. **Concentration risk**: AI chips = <0.2% of units but ~50% of industry revenue — extreme value concentration makes industry fragile

### D) Unique Insights
1. **The depreciation time bomb**: Hyperscalers face ~$400B annual depreciation on AI assets (at ~20% depreciation rate) — this exceeds their combined profits. Either AI revenue must materialize at massive scale, or capex gets rationalized. This is the single biggest risk to the WFE "giga-cycle" thesis
2. **Memory price feedback loop**: 4x price increase → consumer demand destruction → potential inventory correction → memory capex cut. This is a well-documented cycle pattern that current "AI is different" narrative ignores
3. **Data center power constraint**: 92 additional gigawatts needed by 2027 — power availability, not chip demand, may become the binding constraint on AI capex. This would cap equipment demand regardless of willingness to spend
4. **GPU lead times at 30+ weeks**: Supply constraints mean hyperscaler capex plans may not translate 1:1 to equipment orders — some spending is on land/buildings/power, not equipment

### E) Points of Disagreement
1. **Cycle or structural?**: "Giga-cycle" thesis (Creative Strategies, SEMI) says AI breaks traditional boom-bust → sustained growth through 2027+. Gartner projects "cyclical pause in 2028." Seeking Alpha analysis warns capex cycles always mean-revert. The disagreement is fundamental to whether equipment stocks deserve structural growth multiples or cyclical discounts
2. **Hyperscaler sustainability**: Bulls point to $602B capex commitments as "locked in" demand. Bears (Morgan Stanley, BofA) highlight negative FCF at Amazon, rising CDS spreads, and $121B debt issuance as signs the spending pace is unsustainable. Jensen Huang claims $600B annual capex is the new normal; skeptics see it as peak euphoria

### Strongest Bull Case for AMAT
