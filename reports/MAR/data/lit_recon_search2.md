### Why the Valuation Gap?

**HLT premium over MAR (~45% on P/E, ~35% on EV/EBITDA):**
- ~90% of Hilton hotels are franchised (vs. MAR ~98% franchised/managed but with more management-heavy fee mix); investors reward Hilton's franchise-fee-dominant revenue as higher quality/more recurring
- Higher gross margin (30.2% vs MAR 21.9%)
- Higher net unit growth rate (6.7% vs MAR 4.3%)
- Stronger momentum score (74 vs MAR 66 per Zacks)
- Positive earnings revisions for HLT vs downward revisions for MAR
- Better risk-adjusted returns (Sharpe 0.64 vs MAR 0.34)

**MAR discount despite similar model:**
- Marriott's ROIC (11.24%) does not cover its WACC (11.70%), whereas Hilton's ROIC (13.53%) exceeds WACC (10.32%)
- Larger absolute scale can mean slower incremental growth
- Revenue includes cost reimbursement revenue (~$17B of the $26B total), which distorts margins and multiples when using total revenue

**IHG discount to both:**
- Smaller loyalty base (160M vs MAR 271M / HLT 243M)
- Midscale-heavy portfolio limits pricing power
- Lower pipeline (342K rooms vs MAR 610K / HLT 520K)
- LSE-listed with USD ADR adds liquidity/accessibility discount
- IHG P/E of 26-30x (depending on source/timing) represents a discount to both MAR and HLT
