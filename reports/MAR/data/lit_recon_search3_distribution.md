## 1. Direct Booking vs OTA: Major Chain Comparison (2024-2025)

### Marriott (MAR)
- **Bonvoy member penetration**: 68% globally, **75% in U.S. & Canada** (Q4 2025 earnings) [Source: [Hotel Dive](https://www.hoteldive.com/news/marriott-adds-more-than-40-million-loyalty-members/811934/)]
- **Historical direct share**: 76.3% of total room nights (2021 10-K), up 340bps YoY, driven by voice-to-digital migration [Source: [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/Marriott-direct-booking-share-versus-OTA)]
- **OTA share**: ~14% of room nights (2021 baseline) — remainder is GDS/group/other [Source: [Marriott 2022 Annual Report](https://marriott.gcs-web.com/static-files/c0fb865c-15b1-4166-9846-49b548da1ce4)]
- **App growth**: 70% increase in direct sales on Bonvoy app, OTA bookings declined 100bps YoY [Source: [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/Marriott-direct-booking-share-versus-OTA)]
- **Leisure mix**: 44-45% of global room nights (Q4 2024 — Q4 2025), group and business transient ~25% each

### Hilton (HLT)
- **Hilton Honors members**: 243 million (Q4 2024), **fastest-growing** major program (+147% since 2018) [Source: [Skift](https://skift.com/2025/02/26/hotel-loyalty-race-marriott-leads-hilton-is-gaining-fast-see-the-top-13-programs/)]
- **Loyalty room night penetration**: ~62% (combined Marriott+Hilton average cited in industry data) [Source: [BusinessDasher](https://www.businessdasher.com/hotel-loyalty-program-statistics/)]
- **OTA share**: described as "a relatively small percentage" by CEO Nassetta (Q4 2024 call) [Source: [Hilton Q4 2024 Transcript](https://ir.hilton.com/~/media/Files/H/Hilton-Worldwide-IR-V3/quarterly-results/2025/hilton-worldwide-holdings-inc-q4-2024-earnings-call-transcript.pdf)]
- Hilton Honors projected to surpass Bonvoy membership count by mid-to-late 2026

### IHG
- **IHG One Rewards members**: 145 million (2024) [Source: [IHG Annual Report 2024](https://www.ihgplc.com/~/media/Files/I/Ihg-Plc/investors/annual-report/2024/annual-report-2024-IHG-144ppi.pdf)]
- **Direct/managed revenue share**: ~80-81% from IHG-managed sources [Source: [Hospitality.today](https://www.hospitality.today/article/ihg-direct-business-reaches-80)]

### Industry Averages

## 3. Channel Cost Comparison (Per $200 Booking)

| Channel | Cost as % of Revenue | Net Revenue (on $200) | Cancellation Rate | ADR Profile |
|---------|---------------------|----------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Direct (web/app)** | 4.25-7% | ~$186-$191 | ~18% | Higher |
| **OTA** | 15-30% | $140-$170 | 37-50% | Lower |
| **GDS** | 8-20% (total) | $160-$184 | ~4.6% | Highest (corporate) |

**Direct booking cost breakdown**:
- Payment processing: 2.5-3.5%
- Booking engine software: $50-$500/month
- Website maintenance: variable
- Loyalty program cost: ~2-4% (points liability + redemption) — *not included in most "direct cost" estimates*

[Sources: [Switch Hotel Solutions](https://switchhotelsolutions.com.au/direct-booking-costs-vs-ota-fees/), [Heads on Pillows](https://headsonpillows.com/direct-booking-vs-ota/), [Skift GDS](https://skift.com/2025/05/22/how-gds-commission-shares-impact-global-hotel-and-agency-revenue/)]

**GDS specifics**:
- Travel agent commission via GDS: ~10% of room rate
- Plus transaction fees: small per-booking fee
- Total GDS cost: 8-20% depending on structure
- TMC commissions through GDS: $2.1B industry-wide in 2024
- GDS bookings deliver 2-3x more revenue per reservation (corporate travelers, longer stays)
- GDS cancellation rate 4.6% — far superior to OTA's 37%+

**Critical insight**: The ~20pp spread between direct (5%) and OTA (25%) on a $150 ADR = ~$30/night margin difference. For MAR's ~500M room nights/year, even a 1pp shift from OTA to direct = ~$150M annual value to the system (owners + MAR).

## 4. Marriott Direct Booking Strategy

### Best Rate Guarantee (BRG)
- If a lower rate is found on a non-Marriott site within 24 hours of booking, Marriott matches and gives **25% discount** or 5,000 Bonvoy points [Source: [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/travel/marriott-best-rate-guarantee-what-you-need-to-know)]
- 2025 update: minimum rate difference now 1% (same currency) or 2% (different currency), up from $1 [Source: [LoyaltyLobby](https://loyaltylobby.com/2025/05/29/marriott-tightens-best-rate-guarantee-requirements/)]
- Excludes: Vacation Club, all-inclusive, Homes & Villas

### Member-Only Rate Strategy
- Bonvoy members get exclusive "Member Rate" on direct channels
- Additional perks: complimentary Wi-Fi, points earning (0.85 cents/point per TPG Feb 2025 valuation)
- Elite benefits (Platinum+): 50% points bonus, guaranteed late checkout, lounge access, upgrades
- **2024 change**: Marriott stopped giving elite benefits for OTA reservations — strong push to direct [Source: [View from the Wing](https://viewfromthewing.com/change-without-notice-marriott-elites-no-longer-receive-benefits-when-booking-through-an-ota/)]

### Digital & AI Investments
- Natural language search on marriott.com and Bonvoy app planned for H1 2026
- Partnerships: Google AI mode travel planning, OpenAI Ad Pilot program
- Business Access by Marriott Bonvoy: launched mid-2024 for SMB direct booking [Source: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/marriott-mar-leans-bonvoy-ecosystem-231319972.html)]

## 5. Bonvoy 271M Members: What It Translates To

### Membership Scale (Q4 2025)
- **271 million** Bonvoy members (+43M added in 2025 alone) [Source: [CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/marriott-adds-more-than-40-million-loyalty-members/811871/)]
- 143 members per room — #2 in density behind only Hilton [Source: [Skift](https://skift.com/2025/10/10/hotel-loyalty-race-top-14-programs-ranked-by-members-per-room/)]
- Global member penetration: 68%; **U.S./Canada: 75%**
- Many accounts likely inactive — average engagement (nights/member) declining from historical highs

### Economic Translation
- Member penetration 68-75% means ~2/3 to 3/4 of room nights involve Bonvoy members
- Direct booking cost ~5% vs OTA ~15-25% = **10-20pp margin advantage per member-direct booking**
- Credit card economics: co-brand cards (Amex, Chase) generate revenue from card fees even without stays
- Loyalty cost to system: estimated 2-4% of revenue in points liability — but drives repeat booking and higher ADR

### Competitive Context

| Chain | Loyalty Members | Member Penetration | Direct Share |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Marriott Bonvoy** | 271M (Q4 2025) | 68% global / 75% US | ~75%+ (est.) |
| **Hilton Honors** | 243M (Q4 2024) | ~62% (est.) | not disclosed |
| **IHG One Rewards** | 145M (2024) | not disclosed | ~80% |

*Note: IHG's 80% "direct/managed" includes GDS managed through IHG systems, not purely web/app direct. MAR's 75% figure (2021) also includes voice/call center. Definitions vary.*

## 6. Key Quantitative Takeaways for MAR Analysis

1. **MAR direct channel advantage**: ~75% direct vs 14% OTA vs ~11% GDS/other — best-in-class among large chains
2. **OTA commission savings**: Every 1pp shift from OTA to direct saves ~$30/room night on ~$150 ADR
3. **Bonvoy as moat**: 271M members + 75% US penetration + eliminating OTA elite benefits = structural flywheel
4. **Industry trend favors MAR**: Direct projected to overtake OTA by 2030 ($409B vs $333B); MAR already ahead
5. **Channel cost gap widening**: OTA commissions rose from ~10% to 15-30% over past decade; direct costs stable at 4-7%
6. **Risk**: Membership inflation (271M members but declining engagement/member) — quantity vs quality question
7. **GDS underappreciated**: 4.6% cancellation rate vs OTA's 37-50% = GDS room nights far more "real" revenue
