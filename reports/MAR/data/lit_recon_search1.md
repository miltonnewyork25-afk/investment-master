### Fee Structure Detail

- **Base management fees**: Percentage of managed hotel revenues (typically ~3% of gross hotel revenue for luxury/premium, ~2% for select).
- **Franchise fees**: Includes royalty fees (typically 5-6% of room revenue) + **co-branded credit card fees** + application/relicensing fees.
- **Co-branded credit card fees (FY2025)**: $716M, up 8% YoY. Recognized within franchise fees.
- **Residential branding fees (FY2025)**: $72M, down 10% YoY.
- **Incentive management fees**: Performance-based, tied to managed hotel profitability (typically ~20% of hotel operating profit after owner priority return). International managed hotels contribute ~2/3 of total IMFs.
- **Cost reimbursements**: Pass-through with no markup; covers property-level programs and centralized services for owners. Revenue recognized simultaneously with expense — net zero impact on operating income.

### Fee Revenue Drivers (Q4 2025 commentary)
- Co-branded credit card fees: +8% (strong international growth, especially Japan, UAE).
- U.S. & Canada IMFs: +30%+ (led by NYC and Florida resorts).
- Residential branding fees: -20% Q4 (timing of unit closings).

## 4. Revenue Reclassification / Accounting Changes

### Q4 2025 Reclassification
In Q4 2025, Marriott reclassified certain expenses from "General, administrative, and other" to "Owned, leased, and other expense." Prior periods restated for comparability.

**What moved**: Guarantee expense, provision for credit losses, certain brand-related/property-related expenses, and costs associated with certain third-party agreements.

**Impact on reported numbers**:

| Line Item | Q4 2025 As Reported | Q4 2025 Pre-Reclassification |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------------------------|
| G&A expense | $241M | $306M |
| Owned/leased/other (net) | $41M | $106M |

- Full year 2025 G&A: $870M (as reported) vs ~$1.03B (pre-reclassification).
- Excluding $23M Sonder-related charges, pre-reclassification G&A would have been ~$1.0B, down 6% YoY.

### Sonder-Related Charges
- $23M expense in Q4 2025 related to termination of licensing agreement with Sonder Holdings Inc.
- Excluded from adjusted results.

### No Other Major Accounting Changes
- No change to revenue recognition policy for fees.
- Cost reimbursement revenue continues to be recognized net zero (pass-through, no markup).

Source: [Marriott Q4/FY2025 Press Release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/marriott-international-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results-302683152.html), [Motley Fool Earnings Call Transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/10/marriott-mar-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/)
