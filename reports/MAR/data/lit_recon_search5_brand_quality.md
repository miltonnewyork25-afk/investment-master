## 1. J.D. Power 2025 NAGSI Study (Released July 2025)

Study redesigned for 2025; 39,219 responses; 102 brands across 9 segments; 1,000-point scale.

| Segment | Winner | Score | Marriott Brand Placement |
|---------|--------|-------|--------------------------|
| **Luxury** | The Ritz-Carlton (MAR) | 779 | **#1 — segment leader** |
| **Upper Upscale** | Omni Hotels & Resorts | 731 | MAR brands not top-ranked |
| **Upscale** | Drury Hotels | 738 | MAR brands not top-ranked |
| **Upscale Extended Stay** | Hyatt House | 705 | — |
| **Upper Midscale** | Hampton by Hilton | 694 | MAR brands not top-ranked |
| **Midscale** | Tru by Hilton | 723 | — |
| **Economy** | Microtel by Wyndham | 619 | — |

**Key metrics**:
- Average problem rate: 12% across all stays
- Problem impact: satisfaction drops 217 points (677 → 460) when issues occur
- Mobile app users: 699 avg score vs 631 for non-users (+68 point lift)
- Record ADR: $158.67 in 2024, yet value perception improved across all segments

**Takeaway for MAR**: Ritz-Carlton wins luxury, but Marriott's massive midscale/upper midscale portfolio (Courtyard, Fairfield, etc.) faces Hilton dominance — Hampton, Tru, Home2 all segment leaders.

## 4. Starwood Integration — 8 Years Post-Merger (2016-2025)

### Portfolio Status
- 10 brands acquired: St. Regis, Luxury Collection, W, Westin, Le Meridien, Sheraton, Tribute Portfolio, Four Points, Aloft, Element
- All brands retained (no eliminations) — unusual vs. prior industry mergers
- Marriott Bonvoy unified loyalty from 3 legacy programs (Marriott Rewards + Ritz-Carlton Rewards + SPG)

### Brand-Level Performance

| Brand | Status | Key Data |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **St. Regis** | Growing | Expanded from single property to global luxury collection; strong pipeline |
| **Westin** | Stable | 235 properties, 40+ countries; wellness positioning ($6.8T wellness market tailwind) |
| **W Hotels** | **Deteriorating** | Cornell: Strong → Weak (2022→2023); -3.6% CAGR decline |
| **Sheraton** | Weak | Cornell: classified as Weak Brand in upper upscale |
| **Aloft** | Underperforming | ACSI lowest MAR brand at ~74 |

### Integration Challenges
- SH Hotels rebranded as "Starwood Hotels" in 2025 (Barry Sternlicht retained the name post-merger)
- Brand differentiation challenge: 30 brands requires each to be distinct — Tina Edmundson (MAR global brand officer) managing overlap
- Lifecycle hotels (W, Aloft) showing signs of brand fatigue

## 6. NPS (Net Promoter Score) Data

### Marriott NPS (Comparably)
- **Overall NPS: 15** (49% Promoters, 17% Passives, 34% Detractors)
- Ranked **#4** among hotel competitors
- Rankings: Four Seasons (#1) > Hyatt (#2) > Hilton (#3) > **Marriott (#4)**
- Product Quality Score: 3.5/5.0

### Demographic NPS Splits
- Female customers: NPS +4
- Male customers: NPS -7
- Highest: ages 18-25; Lowest: ages 61-65
- Highest: 1-2 year users; **Lowest: 10+ year users** (loyalty fatigue signal)

### Industry Context
- Hospitality industry average NPS: **44** (Retently benchmark)
- Marriott's 15 is **29 points below industry average** — significant gap
- Marriott ranked #79 in Global Top 100 Brands, #9 in Travel & Hospitality

## 7. Customer Complaints & Brand Standards Enforcement

### Quality Audit Gap (2020-2024)
- Marriott suspended pre-pandemic quality inspections for ~3-4 years during/post-COVID
- Only recently (2024) re-imposed brand standard audits
- Former GM (45 years experience): "Marriott stopped doing inspections, provided guest satisfaction scores stayed above a certain level"
- **Result**: 3-4 years of minimal quality enforcement across franchise network

### Franchise Model Quality Risks
- Most MAR franchisees use third-party operators (Aimbridge, Remington)
- Cost-cutting incentives misaligned with brand standards
- Aimbridge described as "notorious for saving money by not doing maintenance"

### Documented Complaints (2024-2025)
- Ambassador member ($200K+ lifetime spend, 100+ nights/year) threatened with account closure for "complaining too much"
- ConsumerAffairs (Nov 2025): "nearly all Marriott branded properties I visited have declined" — worn linens, soiled bedding, nonfunctional wifi, indifferent staff
- Multiple lifetime Platinum/Titanium members report leaving program
- Trustpilot: Mixed reviews with complaints about inconsistent standards across franchise network

### FTC Data Security Settlement (Dec 2024)
- 3 major data breaches affecting 344 million+ customers
- $52M civil penalty (49 state AGs + DC)
- FTC consent order imposing new security requirements

### Leadership/Cultural Shift
- Quality decline attributed to post-J.W. Marriott Jr. retirement + Arne Sorenson death (2021)
- Perceived loss of founding culture: "service, quality, efficiency, professionalism"

## 8. CBRE Brand Performance Context (2024-2025)

### RevPAR Trends
- Nominal RevPAR: +9.3% since 2019
- **Real (inflation-adjusted) RevPAR: -10.9% since 2019** — inflation has fully eroded gains
- Luxury: only chain scale with accelerating RevPAR growth (1.6% → 1.3%)
- MAR ADR increased most among Big 4 (+2.8% in 2024); Hyatt occupancy increased most (+2.2%)

### Brand Proliferation Risk
- Major companies added brands at 7% CAGR; loyalty members at 15% CAGR over 10 years
- **Fastest-growing brand family by count (+15% CAGR) had slowest median RevPAR CAGR (0.3%)**
- Performance gap widening: only 28% of brands outperform sample average (down from 52% pre-2019)

## Synthesis: Key Quantitative Takeaways for MAR Analysis

| Metric | Marriott | Hilton | IHG | Hyatt | Signal |
|--------|----------|--------|-----|-------|--------|
| ACSI 2025 | 78 | 80 | 79 | n/a | MAR trails by 2pts |
| ACSI trend | -1 (declining) | -1 | +1 | n/a | IHG gaining, MAR losing |
| Brand Trust Rank | #2 | #1 | — | #3 | Hilton dominant |
| NPS (Comparably) | 15 | Better (ranked #3) | — | Better (ranked #2) | MAR worst among Big 4 |
| J.D. Power Luxury | #1 (Ritz-Carlton 779) | — | — | — | Luxury strength |
| J.D. Power Midscale | Not top | #1 (Hampton, Tru, Home2) | — | — | Hilton dominates volume segments |
| Cornell Luxury | Ritz=Strong, W=Weak | Waldorf=Strong | IC=Weak | — | W deteriorating |
| Cornell Upper Upscale | Sheraton=Weak | Canopy=Strong | — | Weak | Sheraton drag |
| Quality Audit Gap | ~3-4 years suspended | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown | Franchise risk |
| 10+ year user NPS | Lowest cohort | — | — | — | Loyalty fatigue |

**Bottom line**: Marriott's luxury apex (Ritz-Carlton) is best-in-class, but its massive franchise-driven midscale/upper midscale portfolio shows quality erosion, trailing Hilton on most satisfaction metrics. The 3-4 year audit suspension, NPS of 15 (vs. 44 industry avg), and declining ACSI scores represent tangible brand quality headwinds. W Hotels and Sheraton — two flagship Starwood acquisitions — are classified as Weak Brands by Cornell research.
