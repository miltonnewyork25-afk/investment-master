## 7. OI/ADV Ratio Analysis (Computed)

The OI/ADV ratio (also called "holding period" or "turnover inverse") measures how long the average position is held. Higher ratio = more hedging/structural positions; lower ratio = more speculative/day-trading.

**Interest Rate Futures (best data available):**
- Total IR Futures OI: ~40M contracts (Aug 2025)
- Total IR ADV: 14.2M contracts (2025)
- **OI/ADV Ratio: ~2.8x** — positions held ~2.8 days on average

**SOFR Futures specifically:**
- SOFR OI: ~13.7M contracts
- SOFR ADV: 5.4M contracts
- **OI/ADV Ratio: ~2.5x**

**Interpretation**: An OI/ADV ratio of 2.5-3.0x for interest rates suggests a healthy mix of hedging and trading activity. Pure day-trading markets (like crypto spot) have ratios near 1.0x or below. Pure hedging markets (like long-dated energy) can have ratios of 5-10x+.

**OI Growth vs ADV Growth comparison (Interest Rates):**
- IR OI grew from ~30M (est. 2022) to 40M (Aug 2025) ≈ +33%
- IR ADV grew from ~10.8M (2022) to 14.2M (2025) ≈ +31%
- **OI and ADV growing at similar rates** → suggests balanced growth, not purely speculative layering

**Caution — Basis Trade Factor:**
- The Treasury basis trade (cash-futures arbitrage by hedge funds) is estimated at >$1 trillion notional (May 2025)
- NY Fed: "large, leveraged, and growing"
- Leveraged fund net short futures positions have accompanied growth in Treasury repo borrowing
- This inflates OI without necessarily reflecting end-user hedging demand
- Fed April 2024 Financial Stability Report and CFTC MRAC both warn of systemic risk

## 10. Key Analytical Conclusions

### (a) OI Absolute Levels Summary
- **SOFR**: 1M (2021) → 13.7M (2025) = 13.7x growth in 4 years (includes Eurodollar conversion)
- **Treasury F+O**: ~25M (est. 2022) → 36.3M (Feb 2026) = ~45% growth
- **Total IR**: Record 40M contracts (Aug 2025)
- **E-mini/WTI**: Insufficient public data for year-by-year trend; CME DataMine needed
- **Crypto**: From near-zero to $25B avg daily OI — fastest growing category

### (b) OI/ADV Ratios
- IR overall: ~2.8x (healthy)
- SOFR: ~2.5x (healthy, slightly more trading-oriented)
- The ratio has been relatively stable, suggesting OI growth is not purely speculative stacking

### (c) OI Growth vs ADV Growth: Does It Signal Speculative Activity?
**Mixed signal.**
- At the aggregate level, OI and ADV growth rates are broadly similar (~30% each over 2022-2025 in interest rates), suggesting balanced growth.
- **BUT the basis trade is a confounding factor.** The >$1T basis trade inflates both OI (hedge funds short futures) and ADV (rolling positions). This is technically "hedging" (the cash-futures arb), but it's highly leveraged (50-100x) and concentrated. It inflates OI quality metrics while creating tail risk.
- **Record LOIH (3,526 large holders)** partially offsets this concern — more diverse participants.
- **Asset manager FX OI up 32% in 2025** — genuine institutional hedging growth.
- **New products (credit futures, AIR, battery metals)** driving OI breadth, not just depth in existing products.

### Bottom line for CME valuation:
1. OI growth is REAL and broad-based, but partially inflated by basis trade leverage
2. The quality of OI (measured by participant diversity and asset manager growth) is improving
3. Interest rates dominate (~50% of ADV) — if Fed cuts aggressively and basis trade unwinds, OI could drop materially
4. Energy OI appears relatively stagnant vs interest rates — structural headwind or data gap?
5. The SOFR franchise is the crown jewel — successfully absorbed Eurodollar and continues growing organically
