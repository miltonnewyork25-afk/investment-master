## 7. Key RPC Observations

1. **Interest Rate RPC flat at ~$0.486** — largest asset class, most institutional, least pricing power on per-contract basis. Revenue growth = pure volume story
2. **Equity RPC step-change** — micro contracts (lower notional, higher RPC per dollar risk) shifting mix upward. Retail-driven structural change
3. **Metals RPC declining** — micro Gold/Silver/Copper cannibalizing standard contracts. Volume up but RPC per contract down. Net revenue still growing
4. **Energy & Ag RPC rising** — less micro penetration, institutional dominance, complexity premium
5. **FX RPC steady climb** — OTC-to-futures migration bringing higher-value flow
6. **Total blended RPC $0.707** — up from $0.660 in Q4 2020, +7.1% over 5 years. Mix shift toward higher-RPC products (equity micro, energy) offsetting metals dilution
