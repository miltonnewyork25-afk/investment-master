## Research Limitations & Caveats

### 1. Lock-up Terms — INCOMPLETE DATA
**Issue**: Despite extensive searching, no public disclosure of lock-up terms for NVIDIA or SoftBank investments was found in SEC filings or press releases.

**Implication**:
- Either no lock-up exists (both parties committed long-term)
- Or lock-up terms are in private agreements not disclosed
- Or lock-up is implicit in warrant/governance structures

**Recommendation**: Contact Intel IR directly or review latest 8-K filings for any amendments/details not in initial press releases.

### 2. Hedge Fund Positioning — LAGGED DATA
**Issue**: Hedge fund 13F filings are filed quarterly with 45-day lag. Most recent data reflects Q4 2025 holdings, not real-time positioning.

**Implication**:
- Actual positioning may differ from disclosed 13F
- High-frequency traders/short-term hedgers not captured
- "SA Capital" position from Motley Fool article (Nov 2025) may have changed

**Recommendation**: For current positioning, cross-reference with latest filed 13F (likely filed in early Feb for Q4 2025) and recent news on hedge fund activity.

### 3. Insider Trading — SPARSE VOLUME
**Issue**: Only $249,985 in insider buys over 90 days is minimal for a $230B market cap company.

**Implication**:
- Could indicate either no insider conviction OR blackout windows/regulatory restrictions
- Lip-Bu Tan blackout windows likely during CEO transition period
- Vesting/ESOP exercise transactions are required disclosure but don't indicate conviction

**Recommendation**: Monitor for Q1 2026 insider activity post-blackout windows for clearer conviction signals.

### 4. Analyst Ratings — BACKWARD-LOOKING
**Issue**: Analyst price targets are updated quarterly, typically after earnings. Recent 84% stock rally in 2025 may not be fully reflected in targets published before that.

**Implication**:
- Consensus targets ($43-48) may be stale vs. current sentiment
- Street was bearish on Intel through 2024; recovery narrative still being recalibrated
- Wide dispersion ($25-$65) suggests analyst community still forming consensus

**Recommendation**: Cross-reference with most recent analyst notes (not just price targets) for latest thinking.
