## Key Cross-Company Strategic Insights

### Insight 1: "Bipolar + Fault Line" Market Structure
T1 (ASML + KLAC) separated from T2 (LRCX) by 1.15-point cliff, T2 from T3 (AMAT) by 1.31-point cliff. Within T1, the 0.13-point gap is statistical noise -- choosing between them is preference (certainty vs efficiency), not quality.

### Insight 2: Moat "Shape" Matters More Than "Height"
ASML has the deepest moat (8.12) but also the most extreme shape (std dev 1.68). In 95% of time, height wins. In 5% tail events, shape determines survival. Risk-adjusted, "platform-type" (KLAC, std dev 1.06) may outperform "spike-type" (ASML).

### Insight 3: KLAC Is the "Silent Champion"
In every dimension of risk-adjusted analysis -- probability-weighted returns, regret minimization, Bear scenario protection, scenario stability -- KLAC ranks #1. But market cap is only 34% of ASML and 64% of LRCX. The gap is attributable to "AI narrative bias" (market pays for HBM/EUV stories, not for efficiency/quality compounding).

### Insight 4: LRCX Is a Directional Bet, Not a Quality Hold
