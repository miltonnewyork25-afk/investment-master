### McKinsey — $6.7 Trillion by 2030
- Data centers projected to require **$6.7 trillion** worldwide by 2030
- **$5.2 trillion** specifically for AI-related data centers
- Source: McKinsey "The Cost of Compute" report

### Bain — $500B/year Sustainable If Revenue Supports
- Building data centers with needed compute requires **~$500B capital investment/year**
- $500B/year capex corresponds to **$2 trillion annual revenue** needed (sustainable ratio)
- **$800B gap**: Even if all on-premise IT + 20% AI savings were redirected, still $800B short
- **Bull case**: AI could unlock new value (drug discovery, autonomous vehicles, logistics) to produce the needed revenue

### Bull Case Arguments (NOT a Bubble)
1. **Demand exceeds supply**: Alphabet cloud backlog surged 55% sequentially to >$240B; Microsoft $80B Azure backlog unfulfilled due to power constraints
2. **Productivity math**: 1-2% margin uplift from AI = **$1 trillion incremental earnings** → justifies $10T investment base
3. **Real ROI evidence**: Healthcare AI showing 3.2x ROI; manufacturing 61% report decreased costs from AI supply chain
4. **Efficiency gains**: Alphabet reduced Gemini serving costs 78% through model optimization in 2025
5. **Not speculative**: Unlike dot-com, these are profitable companies spending operating cash flow (though increasingly using debt)

### Bear Case Concerns
- MIT: 95% of organizations getting zero return on GenAI investment
- NBER (Feb 2026): 90% of firms report no AI productivity impact
- OpenAI: $1.4T committed over 8 years vs $13B revenue
- Amazon FCF projected negative ($17B) in 2026 from CapEx
- Circular financing risk: NVIDIA invests in cloud providers who buy NVIDIA chips

## 5. NVIDIA $2B Investment in Lumentum — Precise Terms

### Transaction Summary (SEC 8-K, filed March 2, 2026)
- **Instrument**: Series A Convertible Preferred Stock
- **Shares issued**: 2,876,415
- **Price per share**: $695.31
- **Total consideration**: $2,000,000,000 cash
- **Legal basis**: Section 4(a)(2) of Securities Act of 1933 (private placement)
- **Filed**: Certificate of Designation with Delaware Secretary of State

### Conversion Conditions
1. **At holder's option**: NVIDIA can convert after expiration/termination of Hart-Scott-Rodino (HSR) antitrust waiting period
2. **Automatic conversion**: Immediately before a "qualified sale" (bona fide sale of the Series A to the Company or a non-affiliate)
3. **Conversion ratio**: One-for-one into common stock

### Voting Rights
- **Votes with common stock** on an as-converted basis on ALL matters
- **EXCEPTION**: Series A Preferred Stock does NOT vote in director elections
- **Protective provision**: Company cannot alter/change powers, preferences, or rights of Series A without majority vote of outstanding Series A shares

### Dividends & Liquidation
- Participates in dividends **on an as-converted basis** (same as common holders)
- Participates in liquidation **on an as-converted basis**

### What the Preferred Stock Does NOT Have
- No preemptive rights
- No redemption rights

### Strategic Agreement (Beyond Equity)
- **Multi-billion purchase commitment** from NVIDIA (nonexclusive)
- **Future capacity access rights** for advanced laser components
- **New U.S. fab** focused on AI data center optics (funded partly by the $2B)
- Focus areas: silicon photonics, optical interconnects, package integration

### Dilution Analysis
- Pre-deal shares outstanding: ~67M (2024) → 71.4M (Dec 2025)
- Series A preferred: 2,876,415 shares (one-for-one convertible)
- **Dilution upon conversion**: ~2.88M / (71.4M + 2.88M) = ~3.9% dilution
- **Conversion blocked** until HSR clearance
- Note: $695.31 conversion price vs current $827 → NVIDIA already in the money by ~19%

### Other Share Count Context
- Q1 FY2026 diluted shares: 78.3M (non-GAAP)
- Q2 FY2026 assumed shares: ~83.5M
- Shares increased 12.85% YoY (from various equity issuances including convertible notes)
