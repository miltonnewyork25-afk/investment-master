## 2. Google OCS (Optical Circuit Switching)

### Google's Internal OCS History
- Google deployed MEMS-based OCS internally for years
- Two use cases: (1) spine-layer replacement, (2) TPU cluster interconnect
- Deployed **tens of thousands** of OCS ports
- Now **migrating from internal to commercial OCS solutions**

### Market Size
- OCS market forecast: **>$2.5B by 2029** (revised 40% higher than Jan 2025 forecast)
- Growth driven by Google's accelerated TPU-based AI cluster rollout
- Cignal AI tracks 20+ OCS vendors

### Lumentum as Primary OCS Vendor
- LITE guided ~**$100M/quarter OCS revenue by end of 2026** (implies ~50% market share)
- **R64 OCS**: 64x64 ports, sampling Q4 CY2025, GA H2 2026 — optimized for GPU interconnect
- **R300 OCS**: 300x300 ports — for spine replacement and AI cluster scale-out
- Google qualifying additional suppliers but supply-constrained in near term

### Expansion Beyond Google
- OCS use cases expanding: scale-up/back-end networks, pooled resources, campus DCI
- Other hyperscalers evaluating OCS implementations

## 4. Chinese Optical Competitor Progress

### Innolight (中际旭创) — 300308.SZ
- **2025 Revenue**: Optical transceiver business RMB 374.57B (+63.67% YoY), = 97.95% of total revenue
- **Overseas revenue**: RMB 346.37B (90.58% of total, +67.20% YoY)
- **Gross margin**: 42.04% overall, 42.61% for optical modules (+7.96pp YoY)
- **800G global market share**: **>40%**
- **1.6T global market share**: **50-70%** — only manufacturer with large-scale mass production of both 800G and 1.6T
- **Key customers**: Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA
- **2026 outlook**: Global datacom optical module market projected at $22.8B
- **Next-gen focus**: 3.2T+, silicon photonics, coherent, CPO

### Chinese EML Chip Breakthroughs
- **Zetta Semiconductor**: Announced mass-producible **100G PAM4 EML** at OECC 2025
  - Uses single-ridge waveguide + single-butt-joint regrowth (simplified vs traditional)
  - InGaAsP MQW optimization for performance + production stability
  - Significant: High-speed EML chips have long relied on imports
  - **Gap vs LITE**: 100G PAM4 vs LITE's 200G/lane — still a generation behind
- Note: Not yet at 200G/lane level needed for 1.6T, but closing the gap
